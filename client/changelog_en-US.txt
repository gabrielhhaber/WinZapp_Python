V3.0.0.0

The highlights of this release are the new Neonize engine, faster and lighter, which connects to WhatsApp without a browser (WPPConnect stays available as the alternative), group calls and call links, and AI improvements: summaries, writing help, message translation (online or offline), and better transcriptions and descriptions. It also brings communities, channels, a contacts list, a reorganized settings window and three new languages.

WHAT'S NEW

- Two connection engines. Neonize connects to WhatsApp without a browser or Node.js, through a local bridge, and is now the engine recommended for general use; WPPConnect stays available as the alternative. On the first launch WinZapp asks which one to use, the pairing window has a "Connection engine" list, and Settings > Connection lets you change it later; changing asks for confirmation and restarts WinZapp on the chosen engine. Each engine keeps its own connection, so switching and coming back loses neither. If you already use WPPConnect you stay on it until you switch, and a one-time notice offers the change. WinZapp no longer ships with Node.js: if you choose WPPConnect, it downloads a checksum-verified Node.js and sets WPPConnect up before connecting. Neonize also works on Windows on ARM and, on the Mac, on Apple Silicon and Intel. The features below work with both engines unless said otherwise.

- Neonize, pairing and history: when connecting you choose how much history to bring (recent messages only, 30 days, 6 months, 1 year, all of it, or a period of your own), and WinZapp keeps asking the phone for the conversations and older messages that WhatsApp leaves out of the first batch; the Home key keeps asking for older messages until there are none. While the phone sends its history, WinZapp says so, asks you to keep WhatsApp open on the phone, and warns when it stops, resumes and finishes. With WPPConnect, after the first sync WinZapp also asks the phone for the chats beyond the first batch.

- Communities: the "New chat" window has a "Communities" button listing the communities you are in, with their description, admins and the groups you have joined. Enter or "Open group" goes to a group; a button opens the one with the most recent activity; a filter shows all groups, the ones you are in or the ones you are not; and you can join a group, ask to join and cancel the request. Admins can add and remove groups, promote and demote admins, deactivate the community and create a new one, and WinZapp only says an action is done once WhatsApp shows it. Communities is also a row of the navigation list (Alt+8). In announcement groups, "Community announcement replies" on the message menu reads and sends the replies. Enter on a community in the chat list opens the Communities panel on that community's groups (an option in Settings > Interface > Chats, on by default).

- Channels: a new Channels window (Alt+9, also in the navigation list) shows the channels you follow or searches WhatsApp's directory by category and view (recommended, trending, popular, new). You can open a channel and read its updates, follow or unfollow it, mute it, copy its link, follow one from a link and create one. In your own channels you can post text, photos, videos and polls, edit and pin updates, choose who can react, read the insights WhatsApp Web shows (from 100 followers on), edit the channel, delete it, and manage admins and invitations. Invitations to follow or administer a channel show in the chat. Showing channels in the chat list is a separate option in Settings > Interface > Chats, off by default.

- Contacts: the navigation list gains "Contacts", with the saved contacts one row per person, even when a contact is kept under two addresses. "Search contacts" filters by name or number, and buttons and the context menu open the conversation, start a voice or video call, show the details, delete, block or unblock the contact, or add a new one. "Enter on a contact" chooses what Enter does.

- Starred messages: a new window (Alt+0, or the "Starred messages" row of the navigation list) lists every starred message of every chat with a search field; Enter opens the chat on the message and Delete removes the star. The Chat info and Group info windows get a "Starred messages" tab with the same actions for that chat. Starred messages now end with the word "Starred", like "Edited" and "Forwarded", and a new option in Settings > Interface > Messages says it at the start of the line.

- Group calls: group chats now have the voice and video call buttons, and an incoming group call rings, is announced by the group's name and can be answered. A call that grew to several people is listed by its people, as on the phone, and a call that went on without you is no longer shown as answered. Calls started on the phone can be rejoined: "Join ongoing voice call" (or video, camera off) replaces the call buttons while a call is available, "Return to call" brings back the call window, and the Calls tab lists ongoing calls, including the ones outside groups. A group of more than 32 people cannot be called on WhatsApp, and WinZapp says so.

- Calls with several people: "Call participants" shows who is connected by name, with the Ctrl+Shift+P shortcut; WinZapp says who joins and who leaves (two options in Settings > Calls, on by default) and the list updates by itself. "Add participants" adds people to a call in progress, from a group's members or from your contacts and conversations, with a field for a typed number; it works with a screen reader, with the same marking keys as the conversation lists and a spoken result.

- Call links: in Calls (Alt+6), "Create call link" creates a voice or video call link that you can copy, send to a chat or join. A call.whatsapp.com link in a message joins its call with Enter or "Join call", and a link opened in a browser also joins in WinZapp when it is the program set to open WhatsApp links. Whoever joins first is the call's admin: people without a WhatsApp account wait in a waiting room, WinZapp says each name, and a button lets them in or denies them.

- Call controls: Ctrl+M mutes or unmutes the microphone from any control of the call window, and the outgoing call window announces when it rings or connects. During a voice call on Windows 11, "Share desktop audio" sends the system sound or the sound of a single application, with or without NVDA, at the volume you choose, and keeps going when you mute the microphone. Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Enter answers a ringing call from any window and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Backspace rejects it or hangs up the call in progress. Stereo microphones and headphones are supported in shared mode, with a mono fallback.

- Calls, extras: individual calls can be translated live with Gemini Live Translate, in one or both directions. With Neonize the other person's voice is played with NetEQ, which smooths network jitter and audio loss, in one-to-one and group calls, WinZapp plays the ringback and connection tones that WhatsApp Web plays and the "Call ended" sound (a new sound event), and the calls you place show in your phone's call history.

- AI summary, as in WhatsApp: the open conversation gains "Summarize unread messages" (Ctrl+Shift+U; the button shows with 10 or more unread messages), the chat list menu summarizes without opening the chat or marking it as read, and the message menu summarizes from a message to the end (Alt+Shift+I) or the selected messages (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+I). It uses the providers set up in Settings > Online AI, opens in a window with follow-up questions and a Copy button, and says it was made by AI. Voice messages go in as text, using the transcription saved or a local or online one you choose, with their own consent, which is asked every time in locked chats.

- Writing help: a button after Emoji, the composer context menu and Alt+Shift+H open a window that corrects, rephrases, changes the tone of your draft with your AI providers (not Meta AI) or translates it. Consent is asked before the draft is sent, the result is previewed before replacing the text, and nothing is sent automatically.

- Message translation: Shift+Enter on a text message, or its context menu, translates it. In Settings > Online AI you choose the provider (your AI providers, Grok, your own OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible address, the translation services LibreTranslate, DeepL, Google Cloud Translation, Microsoft Translator and Google Translate, Ollama, LM Studio or an offline model on this computer), the target language and whether the translation replaces the text, follows it or opens in a window. Automatic translation of received messages works in all chats or only the ones you choose, with a maximum age and a daily limit, and never in locked chats. "Translate from here to the end" (Alt+Shift+Y) and the bulk action translate up to 50 messages at a time.

- Mistral is available for online transcription, image descriptions and PDF text extraction. The default Google Gemini model is now Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, which runs into fewer high-demand errors; a model you picked yourself is kept.

- Voice messages: there is a single record button. Settings > Audio chooses Automatic, Mono or Stereo, and the bitrate (32 to 256 kbps, 64 for mono and 128 for stereo by default). A stereo recording is a real voice message that plays on iPhone, and in Automatic a recording with one silent input goes out in mono so you are heard on both sides.

- Noise reducer: with "Enable microphone noise reduction", Settings > Audio can download a neural model, and a new "Noise reducer settings" window chooses the neural or the common reducer, the processor it runs on (the computer's, an NVIDIA card with CUDA 12 or 13, or Core ML on a Mac), and whether to process while recording or when sending. If it fails, you choose between sending with basic reduction or keeping the message pending.

- Recording the microphone together with the computer's audio now goes out as a voice message (Ctrl+Shift+H), in stereo when both channels carry sound. On the Mac this works on macOS 14.4 and later, and WinZapp says when macOS is waiting for or denied the microphone permission. Voice messages and audio can also be saved as MP3 in Save As.

- A reorganized Settings window: a search field and a list of categories (arrow keys change category, Tab enters its options), with several categories merged and others turned into buttons that open one window each. WhatsApp settings (File menu) opens Settings on its own category. A new Advanced category sets the log level, a fixed port for the local API and "Show every WPPConnect Server update", and has "Export this account's logs...". Every control has its own Alt letter in every language, and Settings opens at once, building each page when you first go to it.

- Keyboard shortcuts: the list is a tree by category with a field that finds commands by the keys you press, and the keys you change now apply to every account (a box keeps them for one account only). New commands: Ctrl+Alt+Shift+F1 to F10 assign quick chats that Ctrl+Shift+F1 to F10 open from anywhere, ten commands react to the focused message with the reaction in position 1 to 0 of Settings > Interface > Reactions (without a key, for you to choose), and Alt+Up and Alt+Down move items in the AI provider list and in the options of a new poll.

- Drafts: text typed and not sent stays with the conversation it was written in, mentions included, and comes back when you open it; Settings > Interface > Chats lets you keep it for the next conversation or discard it instead.

- Chat list and Esc: archiving a chat asks for confirmation, like deleting, with a "Don't show again" box and an option in Settings to turn it back on. In the chat lists Esc clears the search and the filters, and in an open chat with message search the first Esc clears the search, both with an option to turn off. New chat options: continue where you left off in the messages list, remove bookmarks after the first use, say the number of new messages before the chat name, and an option that stops new messages in the open chat from joining the unread separator.

- Notifications: when many messages arrive at once WinZapp speaks and plays only the first three (the limit is in Settings > General > Notifications); an option sets the order of group toasts ("Participant name first" or "Group name first"); two options leave archived and muted chats out of the unread count in the tray icon and the window title; "Announce even when WinZapp is not focused" is off by default, so WinZapp no longer interrupts you while you work in another program; and "Notify reactions to your messages" can be turned off.

- Away mode: after a period without a key press or a click in WinZapp (2 minutes by default, in Settings > General > Notifications) you show as offline and messages arriving in the open conversation stay unread until you come back. A call, a recording or audio playing counts as being present.

- Sounds: Sound events gains "Edit of your message saved" and "Message edited by someone else", with their own spoken options per conversation, typing notifications with an optional repeating sound (off by default, silenced while recording and in calls), a "new message" sound choice for the open conversation and for the others, and the sounds and calls set to "system default" follow Windows' default playback device. The installer has a "Sounds to install" group and the update window has "Additional settings" (Alt+A) to keep only the original WhatsApp sounds, which now includes the sounds WhatsApp plays when recording, sending and receiving messages. Imported packs and your own sounds are never touched.

- Pinned messages: the "Pinned messages" window now has the message list's context menu and shortcuts. "Unpin selected" unpins the selected messages at once (Ctrl+Shift+P pins or unpins them with bulk shortcuts on), and an option in Settings > Accessibility > Spoken content stops WinZapp saying how many messages are pinned when a chat opens.

- Messages: Copying selected messages also copies their documents, audio, images and videos, which can be pasted together under their original names; Ctrl+C on a file in the Media tab copies the file; copying only the caption says "Caption copied"; "Message text" (Alt+C) has "Wrap long lines"; and the Open button opens files under their real name. "Try sending again" (Ctrl+Shift+Y) resends a message or status that failed or was not confirmed; nothing is resent on its own. Round video messages show as videos, and a message with a link keeps its link preview when forwarded, with the screen reader saying when a preview is loading, added or not available.

- Business messages: the buttons of a message are answered (reply, quick reply, template buttons and list rows send the answer, links open, copy buttons copy the code, call buttons say the number), orders can be opened with their items, and a buyer can confirm an order paid outside the app. Templates keep their text and buttons, and ClickBus shows its trips in full.

- Polls: when creating a poll, "Hide voter names", a time limit (none, 1 hour, 8 hours, 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days) and Move up and Move down for the options are available, and View votes says "Hidden name" for a poll that hides them.

- Status: you can delete a status for everyone with the Delete key or its button; posting is one at a time and a large video is not reported as failed while it uploads; statuses that did not go out stay in the Status tab; and location, poll, contact and other statuses show their proper label.

- WhatsApp links: a wa.me or api.whatsapp.com link opens the chat with that number, even when it is not a contact, with the message from the link ready in the message field. The installed version registers itself for these links on its first launch; the portable one has the option in Settings > General.

- Updates: a new option in Settings > General downloads updates by itself, without asking, and installs them when you exit (Help > Install downloaded update installs at once). The portable version offers, on its first start, to use a copy of the accounts of the WinZapp installed on the computer instead of linking another device, and "Connect all accounts when WinZapp starts" in the account manager opens every paired account in the background.

- Local transcription: with automatic transcription on, "Also transcribe the ones that arrive late" transcribes received messages however old, voice messages that arrive together after the computer slept are no longer skipped, a chat chooses between no automatic transcription, online or local, and on Windows ARM64 whisper.cpp uses the native build, with an optional CUDA 13 one. "Transcribe without the progress window" queues another message instead of refusing it, and only one model is loaded at a time, so switching accounts no longer fills the memory.

- Storage: Settings > Storage gains a Storage manager that shows sizes and coverage, previews the removal of local copies and lets you restore them; cleaned media can be requested again one by one. With Neonize there is no browser profile, so the profile backup is not shown.

- Arabic, Czech and Italian: WinZapp is now available in Arabic (with a complete usage guide and the installer), in Czech (interface, usage guide, installer and release notes, thanks to jaxcze for the translation) and in Italian (the program; the usage guide and these release notes are still in English). They can also be chosen as languages to translate messages into. Spanish now says "chat" throughout, and Arabic uses "تفريغ" for transcription.

- WhatsApp settings > Privacy has the "Keep chats archived" option, and a chat archived on a device that has it off returns to the list on a new message, as on the phone.

IMPROVEMENTS

- Settings opens and WinZapp starts faster: only contacts that changed are rewritten to the database, big debug logs are silenced and half-downloaded Chrome folders are cleaned up. After a long time offline, WinZapp receives accumulated messages without freezing the interface at every database write, and while WhatsApp is still delivering old messages the window title keeps saying "Updating chats" and the list stays still.

- Chats keep up with the other devices: archive, mute, pin, lock, deletion, clearing, disappearing messages, privacy, "Keep chats archived", lists, blocking, contacts, profile photos, group settings and admins, starred and pinned messages, deleted-for-you messages and poll votes update in WinZapp when WhatsApp tells it, without a full sync.

- Many contact presence updates in a row (typing, online) no longer freeze WinZapp or slow the computer down on accounts with many chats, and typing and recording indicators are kept per conversation and stay available when WhatsApp reports the contact as offline. In groups, WinZapp lists everyone typing or recording at the same time.

- Echo cancellation in calls was rebuilt on WebRTC's canceller, the same engine used by Google Meet and WhatsApp: talking through a laptop speaker or with a mixer's loopback no longer makes the other person hear their own voice, even when the echo comes back after more than half a second. It is on by default, also for people who already used WinZapp, and microphone noise reduction in calls uses it too. Recordings of the microphone with the computer's audio also no longer skip or stop with "audio device problem" on slow computers.

- A contact now has a single name: when someone changes their profile name, all their messages, old ones, group ones and the quoted message of a reply show the new name at once, and a saved contact's name is not replaced by their profile name. A new message from a deleted conversation brings it back to the list, and a number typed in a new chat opens the chat under the address WhatsApp knows, with or without the 9th digit of Brazilian mobiles, with no duplicate.

- The chat list uses the full width of the list and shows one row saying so when a filter or search finds nothing. A new reply or sent message no longer appears above the original when the computer's clock is behind WhatsApp's.

- WPPConnect Server was updated to 2.10.99, with WPPConnect 2.4.7 and WA-JS 4.8.5. Reading message history no longer piles up copies in memory, WinZapp closes the browser automatically only when it opened it, waiting for WhatsApp Web to load works while the page is hidden, and media received without a download address is downloaded by the message ID. When disk space runs low (under 4 GB), WinZapp closes WhatsApp safely before the session is lost and connects again by itself once 5 GB are free. While WhatsApp Web syncs a long history, WinZapp says "syncing with WhatsApp..." instead of showing the account as disconnected.

- Long chats and sync: refreshing a chat with thousands of messages no longer freezes the window, "Messages shown when opening a chat" accepts at most 1,000, and WinZapp asks WhatsApp Web to release memory after large batches of messages. History of unknown groups is recovered without freezing the interface, and "Downloading media: X of Y" counts only the media still to download.

- Updates: installing and building run at lower priority, so the screen reader keeps speaking, several accounts wait for each other to close before the installation, an update that was installed keeps ownership after WinZapp closes, a successful update clears an earlier failure warning, and a failed one opens recovery instructions.

- Audio playback: when the sound card was not available as WinZapp opened the audio output, WinZapp restores it when a playback fails and says so if it still cannot play; notification sounds keep working after switching the audio device; and an audio device chosen in Settings that is not connected at startup is reported once, with the system default used meanwhile, instead of a box that held up startup.

FIXES

- Connecting: after File > Disconnect, the "Connect to WinZapp" window opens above the main window, so the QR code is visible. With the latest WhatsApp Web versions an account that was already linked is no longer logged out every time WinZapp starts and restoring the saved browser profile waits for the previous browser to close and puts the original back if it fails. When WhatsApp temporarily refuses pairing codes for a number, WinZapp says so and suggests the QR code.

- Messages and chats: unread conversations no longer disappear from the Unread filter after an update or restart, pinned messages of a group whose pins are old load, blocking a number that is not saved works, and deleting the last message for everyone updates the list preview. When you switch conversations, buttons of the previous message no longer show up with Tab.

- Keyboard: on a Brazilian (ABNT2) keyboard, "/" and "ç" typed in the message list no longer write "Á" and "º" in the message field, a shortcut change reaches the account you switch to at once, and Esc closes the What's New window. Alt+A no longer opens the attach menu (Ctrl+Shift+A still does), several windows had two controls with the same Alt letter, and Save As on a received JSON file saves it.

- Pasting a text file saved as UTF-16 into the message field no longer sends it as audio: it stays a document.

- Global hotkey: with a WinZapp window waiting for an answer, the hotkey brings that window to the front, and AI image descriptions say when the provider's credit ran out. The account log no longer records the WPPConnect session's access code.

V2.1.0.0

This release brings transcription of voice messages on your own computer, with nothing sent anywhere, and online AI transcription and description of voice messages, images, videos, stickers and PDFs.

WHAT'S NEW

- Local transcription with faster-whisper: fixed CUDA library loading on Windows that could prevent graphics-card use and leave later transcriptions stuck. If the libraries cannot be loaded, WinZapp reports the problem before starting GPU inference.

- Your online status now follows the current window even when you switch away quickly, hide WinZapp to the tray or reconnect in the background. A delayed online update can no longer overtake the offline update, and a failed offline update is retried.

- New shortcuts, each one changeable on the Keyboard shortcuts tab of Settings: Alt+8 opens communities from anywhere in the window; Alt+Shift+B opens the catalog and cart of the business in the selected or open chat; and on a WhatsApp Business account, Ctrl+Shift+N creates an order for the contact of the chat. Where they do not apply, WinZapp says why. Enter on an order message opens the order.

- Help > "What's New Since You Last Looked" says what changed in these release notes since anyone on this computer last opened it, in plain sentences: the new items of each section, the items that changed (as they now read) and the ones removed, and a whole version when it is new. It is the same for every account. And the update window's "What's new?" button now shows what the offered version changes in these notes instead of the notes of the version you have; for an alpha version it also lists, for each alpha since yours, the changes GitHub records (the title and number of each pull request).

- Local transcription with whisper.cpp: the "Keep the model loaded in memory (faster-whisper and whisper.cpp)" option now applies to whisper.cpp too. With it on, the model is loaded once and stays in memory (or on the graphics card) between transcriptions instead of being loaded again for every message; until now the option only had an effect with faster-whisper. If the program that keeps the model loaded cannot be used, the transcription is done as it always was.

- In Settings > Local transcription, the new "Transcribe without the progress window: stay on the messages or statuses until the text is ready (the same shortcut cancels)" option, off by default, makes Alt+Shift+T and Alt+Shift+N transcribe without opening the progress window: you stay on the list and the result appears when it is ready. The same shortcut on the same message cancels.

- Reactions: in the quick reactions list, the Left and Right arrows change the skin tone of the selected emoji, when it has one, as they already did in the emoji picker.

- Carts and orders: in chats with a business, the context menu of the chat has the "Business catalog and cart" item, which lists the products of the catalog with price and availability and builds the cart ("Add to cart", "Change quantity", "Remove from cart", "Empty cart"); "Send order" sends the order once, after a question that says what goes and to whom. If the business turned its cart off, the catalog can still be looked at and WinZapp says it takes no orders there. Order messages and order updates now appear in the message list, in the chat preview and in notifications, with items, total and state; the "View order" item of the message menu opens the details. WhatsApp Business accounts also get, in that window, "Accept order", "Decline order" and "Update status" for the orders they receive, and in the chat menu the "Business tools" submenu, with "Create an order for this contact" and "Accept carts in my catalog". These seller options only appear on WhatsApp Business accounts, as in WhatsApp Web. A product a business sends from its catalog now appears with its name, price and description, instead of as an unsupported message.

- Requests to join a group: in a group where you are an admin, the group data window has the new "Pending join requests" button. It lists who is waiting for approval (name or number, how the request came — by invitation link, through the community or added by a participant — and when), and "Approve" and "Reject" answer the selected request. WinZapp asks WhatsApp for the list again after each answer and only announces it as given when the request has left the list.

- Deleting transcriptions from the keyboard: Alt+Shift+Delete deletes the saved local transcription of the focused message (until now only the "Delete transcription" item of the context menu did) and, with messages selected, follows the "Replace shortcuts with bulk actions when selecting chats and messages" option. Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Delete and the new "Delete the saved local transcriptions of the selected messages" item of the "Bulk actions" submenu delete the transcriptions of every selected message, after a single question that says how many. On the Status tab, Alt+Shift+Delete deletes the transcription of the focused status. The "My status" window now also takes Alt+Shift+T, Alt+Shift+N and Alt+Shift+Delete on your own audio and video statuses. All of these keys are listed on the "Keyboard shortcuts" tab and can be changed.

- Local transcription of statuses: audio and video statuses can now be transcribed on your own computer, with the same keys as in chats. On the Status tab, Alt+Shift+T opens the text in the result window (or the transcription already saved) and Alt+Shift+N puts it in the status line of the list and reads it aloud; both keys can be changed on the Keyboard shortcuts tab. Settings > Local Transcription has three new options mirroring the ones for chats: "Automatically transcribe received audio and video statuses locally and put the text in the status line" (off by default), "Enter and Space transcribe audio and video statuses locally into the status line (Alt+Shift+N then opens or plays them)" (off by default) and "Save local transcriptions of statuses with them so they are not redone (cache; deleted together with the status)" (on by default), plus the "Clear saved local transcriptions of statuses" button. The transcription is kept with the status and deleted with it when the status expires. The per-model cache option is the same one chats use.

- Deleting media messages: when you delete a photo, video, audio or document, the confirmation now has the "Also delete the media from your phone" box, checked by default, as in WhatsApp Web. Uncheck it to delete the message and keep the file stored on your phone. Until now WinZapp always deleted the file from the phone as well, without offering the choice. The box appears when deleting one message or several selected ones, for you or for everyone, and does not appear when none of them is media. In Settings > User Interface, the option "When deleting a media message, start with Also delete the media from your phone checked" sets how the box starts.

- Group invitation links: pressing Enter or Space on an invitation link (https://chat.whatsapp.com/...) inside a message, clicking it or pressing Enter on the message itself now opens WinZapp's "Join group by link" window, with the link filled in and the group preview already requested, instead of the browser; you only join if you choose to. The link's context menu offers "Join group by link" and keeps "Open link" for the browser. The window now accepts links as WhatsApp generates them today, ending in "?mode=...", which used to be refused as invalid. In Settings > General, the new option "Let Windows open WhatsApp group invitation links with WinZapp", off by default, registers WinZapp for "whatsapp:" links: when you use the join button on an invitation page opened in a browser or another program, Windows hands the invitation to WinZapp, even when it is closed (the first time, Windows may ask which app to use). Windows gives https links directly only to Microsoft Store apps, which is why the path goes through that page.

- Bulk transcription of the selected messages (issue #469). The Bulk actions submenu has two entries, each with its own shortcut that can be changed in the Keyboard shortcuts tab: "Transcribe the selected messages and show them together" (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+T, like Alt+Shift+T) and "Transcribe the selected messages into their lines" (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+N, like Alt+Shift+N). With the option to replace shortcuts with bulk actions on, Alt+Shift+T and Alt+Shift+N also act on the selection. WinZapp says how many messages will be transcribed, skipped or reused before starting, transcribes them one at a time in the background announcing "message 3 of 12", carries on when one fails and says at the end how many were transcribed, skipped and failed. The same shortcut or the context menu cancels the queue, and the context menu shows every transcription together, with sender, date and time, to copy or save.

- Local transcription now works with videos too: the video's sound is converted to audio and handed to faster-whisper or whisper.cpp. Every option applies to videos: Alt+Shift+T, transcription into the line (Alt+Shift+N), Enter and Space transcribing (with Alt+Shift+N then playing or opening the video), automatic transcription of received ones, the per-model cache and clearing it. GIFs are skipped, and a video with no sound is reported as such.

- In Settings > Local transcription, the transcription cache now has controls of its own: a general option (on by default) to save transcriptions with the messages so they are not redone, a choice for each model (follow the general setting, on or off), and the button "Clear saved transcriptions of all models", which clears every model's cache in every chat. With the cache off for a model, its new transcriptions are not saved and appear in the result window; the ones already saved stay until cleared.

- In Settings > Local transcription, the option "Automatically transcribe received voice messages and put the text in the message line", off by default, transcribes each voice message that arrives with local transcription and shows the text in the message's line, like the inline transcription you make by hand. Transcriptions run one at a time, only for freshly arrived messages (up to 10 minutes of audio) and outside locked chats. No windows or announcements per message; if local transcription is not ready, WinZapp pauses for a while and says why (for example, that no model is ready to use), once: the pause then retries quietly, and the notice comes back only after you apply the settings or a transcription has worked again. A moment when no model fits in the free memory does not pause it.

- In Settings > Local transcription, the group "Reuse downloads from another folder" lets you point WinZapp at the folder of CUDA libraries (cublas64_12.dll and cublasLt64_12.dll) and at the folder of whisper.cpp programs it already downloaded (the one holding the cpu and cuda folders), instead of downloading everything again. The folder is only read: nothing in it is copied, changed or deleted. The CUDA libraries must load, and the whisper.cpp programs go through the same checks as the ones WinZapp downloads.

- In Settings > Local transcription, the option "Keep the model loaded in memory (faster-whisper)", off by default, spares you the wait for the model to load after each pause between transcriptions. Without it, the loaded model is released from memory 5 minutes after the last transcription, giving the computer's (or the graphics card's) memory back to other programs. whisper.cpp starts a new process for every transcription, so the option changes nothing for it.

FIXES

- Fixed a "chat not found" error when sending messages or attachments to an existing conversation already loaded by WhatsApp.

- Receiving a file no longer downloads it into the message notification because of an unused webhook setting. Upload and download progress updates now keep only the latest pending value, avoiding a buildup of screen updates during fast transfers.

- Fixed repeated conversation synchronization when WhatsApp's chat list was available but could not be transferred to the local server. Existing conversations are kept and the list can finish synchronizing.

- Sending voice messages and documents failed with "Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'prepRawMedia')" on a WhatsApp Web build released on 15 October, which moved that function. WinZapp now ships the matching version of its WhatsApp Web library (WA-JS 4.7.1). If sending still fails after updating, reinstall WPPConnect from the update window.

- macOS: in the chat and message lists, VoiceOver no longer loses its place when a row changes, for example when an unread count changes, someone starts recording audio or a new message arrives. Opening a chat with VoiceOver now works at once and every time, instead of sometimes failing or acting only after a pause, and VoiceOver then moves to the message field; notices such as the one about pinned messages are spoken after the field instead of cutting it off.

- macOS: Command shortcuts now do the same thing on every screen. A button's underlined letter used to take over Command and that letter: while "Read more" was shown, Command-R pressed it instead of recording a voice message, and other buttons took other shortcuts the same way. A button's underlined letter is now pressed with Command-Option and the letter, the Mac form of Alt and the letter on Windows, and a WinZapp shortcut on the same keys still comes first, as on Windows.

- The buttons for recording in the other mode (stereo or mono) and for recording with the computer's audio told the screen reader their shortcut was Ctrl+R. They now announce their own, Ctrl+Shift+G and Ctrl+Shift+H, or the keys you assigned to them.

- The Group info, Chat info, Group invitation and Join group by link windows now have an access key (Alt+letter) on every button and on every label of a field or list. Before, the buttons of Group info, some of Chat info and every control of the invitation had none. The button that closes the invitation window, which was shown in English, now follows WinZapp's language.

- Messages sent while a run of voice messages was playing or paused showed no status in the list (no "Sent", "Delivered" or "Read") until the run ended, even though Message info (Alt+Shift+D) already showed the right one. Now, during a run, only the row that has focus waits, so the screen reader does not read it over the audio; every other row is updated right away, and the one that waited is updated when focus leaves it, when playback is paused or when the run ends.

- Updates: "Check for updates" in the Help menu now tells you when WinZapp could not reach the update server, instead of saying nothing.

- Replies: when the original message cannot be found before sending, WinZapp reports the missing quote and keeps your text for editing from the message menu. It does not silently send without the quote or report a connection loss. Uncertain sends are still not retried automatically.

- Synchronization: replayed history messages keep their chronological position instead of appearing as new arrivals. Temporary failures when checking an alternate chat address remain queued for retry.

- Forwarding no longer opens a box when it worked. "Message forwarded." (or how many messages were forwarded) is now said by the screen reader, with no window to close and without cutting off what is being read; it works with WinZapp in the tray too.

- Syncing and backfilling chat history now ask WhatsApp Web for at most two chats' messages at a time instead of up to six, which keeps the processor and memory use low on small computers and servers during the first sync, without making it noticeably slower. Opening a chat, scrolling up and resyncing a conversation are not slowed down.

- The updater now cleans up after itself: when an update has been installed it deletes the folder it extracted into your temporary folder, and the next time WinZapp starts it also removes what earlier updates, failed installs and interrupted downloads left there, which could add up to a full copy of each version you ever installed.

- Ctrl+Shift+V (voice call) and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V (video call) now also work while the message field is focused.

- Sending a message to yourself no longer fails with "could not confirm the send" on accounts whose own address carried a device suffix.

- When you open or read a chat inside WinZapp, the notification for its new message is removed from the notification area instead of lingering there.

- After the computer wakes from sleep, new messages are fetched reliably: the open conversation and recent chats are checked again even when WhatsApp's chat list looks unchanged, and updates for chats linked by a linked-device ID are no longer ignored

- Mentions: a person mentioned by name next to @all keeps their link in the message's mentions, and a mention of you, in the notification and in the chat list preview, uses the word chosen in "How should I be referred to?" instead of a fixed word

- Settings, "User Interface" tab: the tab now scrolls, and the option groups near its end are read in the right place again. On smaller screens the screen reader announced "Position to announce selected items" on the checkbox after it, and not on the group's own radio buttons

- AI media: resetting all keys also clears remembered sending consent, even when a new key is entered before applying. Long transcripts and PDF text now report a text-limit error instead of silently losing their endings; another provider is not tried automatically for this limit.

- "Describe" button on media messages. On a photo, sticker or video (and on audio and PDFs too, when the AI features are on), a button appears right after "Save as", reachable with Tab, that does the same as the Ctrl+Shift+I shortcut; in the context menu the item now sits with the other media actions, also showing Ctrl+Shift+I. - WPPConnect Server updated to 2.10.55. Besides upstream fixes for requests that were answered twice, when WhatsApp Web could not look a number up (for example while the page was reloading), the send is now tried again instead of wrongly reporting that the number does not exist. The same recipient in one request is also looked up only once. If WinZapp offers the WPPConnect update at startup, accept it to install the new version.

- Logging out of a session that was left unfinished (for example an account that never finished pairing) now gets an answer instead of failing silently.

- macOS: installing an update, and the first launch after it, are much faster. - Echo cancellation in voice and video calls now works on calls you answer. - WPPConnect Server updated to 2.10.58. Upstream now refuses to start a session while sessions are being backed up or restored, saves each session's token file atomically so a crash can no longer leave it half-written, and reuses a checked access token for a minute instead of checking it again on every request. The big news in this release is calls: you can now place, answer and reject voice and video calls from WinZapp itself, without picking up your phone, with an accessible call window, keyboard shortcuts, dedicated audio settings and your call history in the new Calls tab. - A warning for an unconfirmed send. When WhatsApp accepts a message but does not confirm delivery, WinZapp tells you to check the conversation before resending, rather than risking the same message arriving twice.

IMPROVEMENTS

- Starting with Windows now connects far sooner. The app used to wait for the connection only after it had already tried to use it, and spent a long time showing "disconnected from WhatsApp" for no reason. It now waits for everything to be ready before appearing in the system tray.

- The message list is no longer rebuilt in full every minute. Only the rows that actually changed are rewritten, so the screen reader stops re-reading the message you are on and the selection sound no longer fires by itself.

- Fewer sync notifications on your phone. WinZapp only asks the phone for older history in conversations you have opened, one request at a time, and stops asking altogether once a conversation has answered that it has nothing more.

- Conversations the sync had stopped looking at are checked again on their own, without you having to refresh with F5.

- With several accounts open at once, a WinZapp update opens a single window instead of one per account.

- Audio: WinZapp asks the microphone for its own sample rate before trying the fixed ones, and sound output recovers on its own when the default device changes or is unplugged.

- Reactions that arrived while a conversation was closed now show up when you open it.

- Line breaks in the message box can now be navigated normally by the screen reader.

- Updated WhatsApp connection (WPPConnect 2.10.21), including security fixes, and WinZapp now notices on its own when that connection has drifted and needs reinstalling.

FIXES

- Shutting the computer down no longer breaks the session. WinZapp closed the WhatsApp connection too late in the Windows shutdown, once the process holding it had already been killed — and the profile came back unusable, asking to pair again next time. It now closes everything while Windows is still asking whether it may shut down.

- Suspending the computer no longer breaks the session. On resume, WinZapp restarted the connection without waiting for the browser to finish writing what it had in hand, and that alone was enough to lose the pairing. It now waits.

- Replying to a status works again. The reply failed saying it could not be sent.

- An ambiguous send failure no longer produces duplicate messages: when there is no way to tell whether WhatsApp received it, WinZapp does not resend on its own.

- Downloading large documents is possible again. Files of a few hundred megabytes announced "downloading" and produced nothing.

- The upload and download progress indicator now reports the transfer that is actually happening, and no longer keeps spinning on a message that has finished or appears on the wrong row.

- Cancelling or closing the pairing screen no longer deletes your conversations, and neither does a momentary authentication failure at startup. History is only cleared when a different phone number is genuinely connected — and WinZapp tells you when that happens.

- The QR code is only announced once it is genuinely on screen. WinZapp used to say "the QR code was updated" before any code had been shown at all, which made the code seem to appear only on the second try. The pairing screen now says the code is being generated, announces it when it actually appears, and says when it could not be generated or when the pairing code has expired — instead of staying silent or announcing an update that never happened.

- Unread counts: conversations no longer come back as unread without a new message having arrived.

- The phantom character that appeared in the message field when returning from another program with NVDA running is gone.

- Copying a voice message now works.

- Conversations that showed a number instead of a name are now resolved.

- Switching accounts now clears the previous account's media properly, which could otherwise be left behind on the computer.

V1.1.0.0

This release adds the Media tab, a media viewer in its own window, link previews, bulk actions and an Accessibility tab in settings. Conversation syncing is much faster, and the conversation list is no longer rebuilt on every message, which cuts down a great deal of screen-reader re-reading.

WHAT'S NEW

- Media tab in conversations and groups. It lists photos, videos, audio files, voice messages, documents and links, with filtering by type and by downloaded or not downloaded, full history loading, and open, save and delete actions on each message. Audio files and voice messages are two separate filters: you can show only one or only the other, and choose whether automatic downloading fetches one, the other or both.

- Media viewer in its own window, with play and pause, skip forward and back 10 seconds, position, volume and speed. You choose in settings whether conversation videos and statuses open in it.

- Link previews in messages. When a message has two or more links, they become a list you can navigate. Your outgoing messages now carry a preview too.

- Bulk actions on messages and conversations: copy, star, pin, forward, save and delete messages; mark conversations read or unread, archive and clear them. Each action has its own shortcut, and Ctrl+C copies the selection.

- Accessibility tab in settings, with extended screen-reader compatibility and the option to use the SAPI voice when no screen reader is running.

- Files and saving tab, to choose whether save dialogs open in the last folder you used, in your Downloads folder, or in a folder you pick.

- Reactions: you can now see which reaction is yours and remove it by selecting it again.

- Save a received contact message as a contact, with Enter or Space.

- Play back the audio you have already recorded while recording is paused, with Ctrl+P.

- Rebuilt country selector in the pairing dialog: it uses the country and language configured in Windows, is sorted alphabetically, accepts multi-letter type-ahead search, and protects the country code from accidental editing.

- New settings: show "yesterday" instead of the date, announce "Forwarded" before the sender, tell voice messages apart from audio files, which is now the default instead of reading everything as audio, determine a video's length as soon as it is downloaded, choose which media types are shown by default, and stop marking audio as played.

- Search in the contact lists ("Attach contact" and "Add member"): the field is focused as soon as the list opens, filters by name (including surname) and by number as you type, and clearing it shows every contact again.

- Received contact card: a context menu with view name and number, copy the number with Ctrl+C, and start a chat. When the card carries more than one number, WinZapp asks which one to use.

IMPROVEMENTS

- Documents can now be up to 2 GB, which is WhatsApp's real limit. WinZapp used to stop them at 1 GB for no reason. Photos, videos and audio stay at 1 GB, which is WhatsApp's limit for those.

- Incremental conversation syncing. Starting the app and refreshing is much faster, and WinZapp announces when the update starts and when it finishes.

- The conversation list is far faster. A new message or a delivery receipt updates only that one row instead of rebuilding the whole list. In practice, the screen reader re-reads far less.

- Actions on a message also update only that message's row, and editing a message no longer freezes the interface.

- Notifications respect Windows Focus Assist.

- Groups: a notice when only admins may send messages, with the message field set to read-only. The add-member dialog shows names instead of numbers and reports who could not be added.

- Attaching a contact: the list has no duplicates, is sorted, and no longer shows unresolved entries.

- Audio: OGG files the app could not decode are now converted automatically, and sending WAV files works.

FIXES

- Session dropped by WhatsApp: WinZapp now says so straight away and opens pairing, instead of leaving you on the conversation list while repeating that the QR code has been updated. With nobody on the pairing screen the session is closed rather than left asking WhatsApp for a new code forever — that endless stream of requests could get the account banned — and WinZapp says out loud that it closed the session and that you need to connect again.

- Unread counts: already-read messages no longer come back as new, a chat read on your phone is respected, and the open conversation no longer loses its count.

- The unread separator and your reading position are no longer lost while you read. The open conversation was being rebuilt periodically, which interrupted the screen reader mid-sentence.

- Session: closing and reopening WinZapp no longer asks you to pair again. Shutdown now waits for the session to be written before closing.

- Pairing: when WhatsApp refuses the code, the real reason is shown, and a server update no longer interrupts pairing in progress.

- Automatic updates work again when the app is installed in a folder with accents or other special characters.

- Privacy: the app no longer writes message content or credentials to the log file.

- Sending: cancelling a message that had already been sent is now explained clearly, and large uploads are no longer lost along the way.

- Videos with no reported length no longer show "0 seconds".

- The first message from a contact no longer disappears when the conversation is opened.

- Accessibility: the emoji shortcut is now reported to the screen reader, Alt+T works inside a conversation again, and speech is no longer cut off by every delivery receipt.

- Syncing no longer restarts over and over. A single chat WhatsApp does not recognise, and that was never a real conversation, was enough to make WinZapp believe the sync had never finished: it started again from scratch every few minutes, announcing itself each time, even with everything already loaded.

- The fast conversation refresh no longer skips chats that received new messages. They stayed on their old contents until you pressed F5 and synced everything, which defeated the point of the faster refresh.

- WinZapp now knows the expiry date of the WhatsApp Web version it uses. When it lapses, it fetches the one WhatsApp is currently serving instead of insisting on one that is no longer accepted. This is what stalled syncing, left the conversation list empty, and ended with WhatsApp dropping the computer from your linked devices.

- The first pairing code after a dropped connection no longer fails. WinZapp was requesting codes on its own, in the background, until WhatsApp refused for too many requests; it now waits between requests, and when WhatsApp does refuse, the message explains why instead of showing an internal error.

- The "not downloaded" media filter was listing voice messages that were already on the computer, including ones you had played inside the app.

- A document you send now shows its file size as soon as it appears in the message list, like received ones already did.

- WinZapp now prefers a stable WhatsApp Web build over an alpha one when both are available — using an alpha was getting the device unpaired hours after pairing. The initial sync is also more tolerant of slowness before it decides the connection is down.

- The "Messages to myself" chat no longer shows delivery and read receipts, or the "played" audio mark — they make no sense in a chat with yourself.

- "Delete for everyone" in the "Messages to myself" chat now actually works. The message used to disappear only locally and come back on the next sync.

- Alt+2, Alt+3 and Ctrl+W with no conversation open now say "No chat is open" instead of doing nothing.

- Alt+2 in an empty conversation says "This chat is empty" instead of staying silent.

- The conversation list returns to the row focus was actually on, not always to the conversation that had been open.

- The unread separator no longer disappears or shows the wrong count when the app is minimized to the system tray with the conversation open.

- An archived conversation is no longer announced out loud when a new message arrives while the app is in the foreground.

- Playing voice messages back to back no longer reads the whole previous row out loud over the new one.

- The option to silence the screen reader while recording audio now stops the announcement at the source, instead of trying to cancel it after it has already started.

- Adding a new contact through WinZapp could duplicate the conversation and leave its first message permanently stuck as "not confirmed" whenever WhatsApp recognized that number in a different format (e.g. with or without the 9th digit).

- A group's name changed on WhatsApp is now picked up by WinZapp, including on an F5 resync. Before, the old name was never replaced.

- Switching the sound output device in Settings while an audio was playing could make the first attempt to play another audio silently fail; it now plays on the first try.

- Disconnecting from File > Disconnect no longer plays the "connected"/"sync started" sounds again or tries to reconnect on its own — the account stays properly disconnected.

- The update installer's log now lives inside the account's own logs folder (alongside log.log and shutdown_audit.log), instead of loose in the program folder.
