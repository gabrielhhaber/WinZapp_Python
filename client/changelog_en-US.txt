V2.1.0.0

This release brings transcription of voice messages on your own computer, with nothing sent anywhere, and online AI transcription and description of voice messages, images, videos, stickers and PDFs.

WHAT'S NEW

- Pinned messages: a button in each chat lists the messages pinned on WhatsApp, including older messages. Press Enter to go to one in the history. Opening a chat announces the number of pinned messages without moving focus; each pinned message also has a readable marker.

- The message field's "Spelling" context submenu offers Windows dictionary suggestions. The editor keeps its standard screen-reader navigation and WinZapp's spelling-error sound.

- "Keep pinned chats in a fixed order" in the User Interface tab of Settings. When enabled, new messages do not move pinned chats, and their order is kept after restarting. It starts off; unpinned chats still rise when new messages arrive.

- Transcribe and describe media with AI. The new "Online AI: Transcriptions and Descriptions" tab in Settings turns the AI features on (they start off) and takes the API key of one or more providers — Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq and OpenRouter — which are tried in the order you set, so when one fails or runs out of quota the next one answers. In a message's context menu, or with Ctrl+Shift+I, "Transcribe with online AI" turns a voice message into text, "Describe" describes a photo or a video, "Describe sticker" describes a sticker and "Convert to accessible text" turns a PDF into plain, readable text. The result opens in a window with the text, and for photos and videos you can ask follow-up questions in it. WinZapp asks before sending media to a provider and asks again every time for locked chats; your keys are encrypted on your computer and are sent only to the provider they belong to. A waiting sound plays while the provider works, and answers can be read aloud automatically. The text follows the language of the app.

- Local transcription of voice and audio messages. "Transcribe message locally with Whisper", in the context menu of a voice or audio message, or with Alt+Shift+T, turns the audio into text on your own computer with Whisper: nothing is sent anywhere. The text opens in a window where it can be copied, saved or inserted into the message, and it is kept with the message, encrypted just like it; afterwards, Alt+Shift+T shows the saved transcription again, and the context menu offers "View transcription", "Transcribe again" and "Delete transcription". Everything is set up in the new "Local Transcription" tab in Settings. There are two transcription components: faster-whisper, the default, which comes with WinZapp, and whisper.cpp, an optional program the tab installs in its processor version or its NVIDIA graphics card version. whisper.cpp is experimental: it has not yet been tested with the final release package, so faster-whisper is the supported component. faster-whisper can also use an NVIDIA graphics card once you install the "CUDA libraries" from the same tab. Models are downloaded only when you ask, in every size from tiny to large-v3 and large-v3-turbo, and, for whisper.cpp, also in smaller 8-bit and 5-bit versions, and some in a larger 32-bit, full-precision one. The list also has models that understand only one language, marked as such: the English-only ones and third-party ones for Swedish, Hebrew, Yiddish and Japanese. "Where to run the transcription" and faster-whisper's "Calculation precision" stay on Automatic or can be chosen by you. A model you have already downloaded to another folder can be used from "Models in other folders" without being copied, and the models folder can be moved elsewhere, models included. A voice filter keeps Whisper from inventing text in the silence at the end of a message; when it cannot be used, WinZapp tells you. This does not replace "Transcribe with online AI": they are two different commands, and only the online AI one sends the audio to a provider.

- A separate sound plays when someone reacts to your message. Change its file or turn it off in Settings > Sound Events.

- WhatsApp custom lists: refresh and filter by your lists, create or rename a list, and add or remove selected chats. List editing depends on WhatsApp account support. Locked and locally unavailable chats are excluded from selection; other memberships remain unchanged. Unverified changes are reported without automatic retries. When editing is unavailable, the manager explains whether WhatsApp has disabled it, this version lacks support or the support check failed.

- Contacts synced with the phone: when you add a contact (in "Chat info", in "New chat" or from a contact message) there are now two tabs, "WinZapp local contact" (only in WinZapp) and "Contact synced with the phone", which saves the name in WhatsApp and in the phone's address book. The synced tab opens by default, a number that is already in the phone's address book shows only that tab, and deleting a synced contact also removes it from WhatsApp and the phone

- New shortcut Alt+Shift+P in the messages list: jump to the previous message that replies to you, like Alt+Shift+M does for mentions. Repeated presses go back through them and wrap to the newest. It is also listed in "Keyboard shortcuts" (F1)

- "Is typing" in the messages list: while the person in the open chat (or, in a group, a participant) is typing or recording audio, a temporary last row such as "Ana is typing..." appears at the end of the messages list. It goes away when they stop or when their message arrives. It never moves the focus on its own (only if you are on the row when it goes away are you moved to the last message) and is not read out on its own, so the typing announcement stays exactly as it was. It is not a message: it has no context menu and no actions. It starts off; to turn it on, tick "Show who is typing or recording audio as the last row of the messages list" in the User Interface tab of Settings

- Emoticons become emoji as you type, like in WhatsApp Web: ":)" turns into 🙂, ":D" into 😃, "<3" into ❤️ and so on, when you type a space or punctuation after them, or send the message right after one. Only an emoticon standing on its own is converted, never one inside a link, a word or `code`. Pressing Backspace right after a conversion puts back what you typed. It can be turned off in Settings, "General" tab, "Convert emoticons like :D to emoji while typing"

FIXES

- Mentions: a person mentioned by name next to @all keeps their link in the message's mentions, and a mention of you, in the notification and in the chat list preview, uses the word chosen in "How should I be referred to?" instead of a fixed word

- Settings, "User Interface" tab: the tab now scrolls, and the option groups near its end are read in the right place again. On smaller screens the screen reader announced "Position to announce selected items" on the checkbox after it, and not on the group's own radio buttons

- AI media: resetting all keys also clears remembered sending consent, even when a new key is entered before applying. Long transcripts and PDF text now report a text-limit error instead of silently losing their endings; another provider is not tried automatically for this limit.

- "Describe" button on media messages. On a photo, sticker or video (and on audio and PDFs too, when the AI features are on), a button appears right after "Save as", reachable with Tab, that does the same as the Ctrl+Shift+I shortcut; in the context menu the item now sits with the other media actions, also showing Ctrl+Shift+I. The button and the item only appear on messages that can be described.

- "Automatic (recommended)" model in each AI provider window (Online AI: Transcriptions and Descriptions). Checked by default for providers where you had not picked a model, the box makes WinZapp always use the model it recommends, which follows updates; uncheck it to pick or type a fixed model.

- Audio files sent as an attachment: a WAV or OGG Vorbis file in stereo or surround (5.1, 7.1) now arrives with all its channels, as ordinary audio, and keeps its sample rate up to 48 kHz (a higher one is sent at 48 kHz). It used to arrive in mono, looking like a voice message. A file with more than 8 channels, which AAC cannot carry, is sent untouched as a document instead.

- Updating WinZapp no longer undoes a WPPConnect update. After updating WPPConnect to a newer version than the one WinZapp comes with (from the update notice or with "Force reinstall WPPConnect" in the Help menu), the next WinZapp update put it back to the old version, and the same WPPConnect update was offered again

- Status: "Like ❤️" works again. After a change in WhatsApp Web, liking a contact's status failed with "Could not send the reaction to this status."

- "Use custom API (remote server)": WinZapp no longer says "The browser profile used to connect to WhatsApp is damaged and there is no saved copy to restore" about a profile that is fine. That profile lives on the server, and WinZapp looked for it on this computer, found nothing and concluded it was damaged. With a custom API, WinZapp now leaves the profile and its backups to the server, and when it closes it no longer stops a WPPConnect on this computer that it did not start.

- Accounts with many chats, whose WhatsApp Web takes a little over 30 seconds to load, now connect. WinZapp gave up waiting at exactly 30 seconds, so the same account failed the same way every time it started.

- WPPConnect update: the previous version is kept until the new one starts and responds, and is restored if it does not. The update is refused before stopping anything when there is not enough disk space for both, and reinstalling reuses the Chrome already downloaded.

- Last seen in an open chat now stays up to date. The conversation button, Alt+T and the contact details show the same information, and an old answer can no longer overwrite a newer online or last-seen update.

V2.0.0.0

The big news in this release is calls: you can now place, answer and reject voice and video calls from WinZapp itself, without picking up your phone, with an accessible call window, keyboard shortcuts, dedicated audio settings and your call history in the new Calls tab. Locked chats, search in archived chats, Turkish and Romanian and several usability improvements arrived too, along with fixes for situations where WinZapp asked you to pair again when it did not need to. Under the hood, the program's code went through a major reorganization: it changes nothing in how you use WinZapp, but it makes it easier to maintain and to fix from here on.

WHAT'S NEW

- Starred messages now use WhatsApp's own stars. Each change is checked on the linked device; failures and unverified results are reported separately. Old local stars remain until you choose "Sync old local stars with WhatsApp" in the chat's context menu and confirm. You can stop a batch after the current request.

- Voice and video calls. In a one-to-one chat, the "Voice call" (Ctrl+Shift+V) and "Video call" (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+V) buttons place the call from WinZapp itself. The call opens a window with Tab-reachable buttons and its own shortcuts: Ctrl+M mutes or unmutes the microphone, Ctrl+V turns the camera on or off in a video call, Ctrl+P turns a voice call into a video call, Ctrl+C opens the call settings and Ctrl+Shift+Q hangs up. The screen reader tells you when the call connects, when it is reconnecting and when it ends.

- Answer and reject calls in WinZapp. The incoming call window now has "Answer", "Reject" and "Silence alert" buttons; for a video call, "Answer with video" and "Answer without video". That window's shortcuts only work with Alt and the underlined letter, so a key pressed by mistake does not answer or reject the call.

- New Calls tab (Alt+6), with the call history of every chat, split into All, Missed, Declined and Answered; Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab switch between them. In the list, Enter opens the call's chat, Ctrl+Shift+R calls back a missed call and F5 refreshes the list.

- Calls show up in the chat and in the chat list, as in WhatsApp: "Missed voice call", "Answered video call" and so on, at the point in the chat where they happened. On a missed call, the "Call back" button or Ctrl+Shift+R calls back.

- Call audio and video settings. In the Calls tab of Settings, "Call audio settings" chooses the microphone and speaker used for calls, and "Call video settings" chooses the camera. Echo cancellation lives there too — it reduces your own sound coming back when you use speakers instead of headphones — microphone noise reduction, which lowers steady noise such as fans and hiss, and exclusive mode for the microphone and for the speaker. During a call, the same options open with Ctrl+C.

- Locked chats (Alt+7). "Lock chat", in the context menu or with Ctrl+Shift+T, moves the chat into a vault protected by a 6-to-12-digit PIN, out of the chat list, the archived chats, search and the forward window. Notifications for those chats only say how many new messages arrived, with no name and no content. When you set the vault up, WinZapp creates a recovery key, which can be read aloud or copied, to set a new PIN if you forget the current one. The vault closes by itself when WinZapp is minimized, hidden to the system tray or switched to another panel, and also, if you want, after a while without use; Ctrl+Shift+K closes and hides the vault at once. Locked chats can also be taken out of the navigation menu: the vault is then opened by typing a secret code into the chat search.

- Search in archived chats. Archived chats (Alt+3) now have a search field, like the main list: Ctrl+F takes you there, and Down Arrow moves to the first result.

- Two new languages: Turkish and Romanian. Choose them in Settings, General tab.

- React with any emoji. "Add more reactions" at the end of the 12 quick choices opens the full picker; emojis you use often gradually take their place among those quick choices, most used first. If you would rather find each reaction in the same position every time, use the new Reactions tab in Settings: with "Use fixed quick reactions" on, the 12 positions no longer change with use, and Enter on a position opens the emoji picker to choose which reaction goes there.

- Show a file saved with Save As in its folder. Once that user-chosen copy exists, use Ctrl+Enter, the "Show in folder" context-menu item or the Tab-reachable button beside Open and Save As to open File Explorer with it selected; internal downloads alone do not expose the action.

- Choose whether starred messages stay when clearing a chat. The clear confirmation now has a "Keep starred messages" option, ticked by default, just like WhatsApp Web. Unticked, starred messages are cleared along with everything else, on your phone too. It applies to clearing one chat, several selected chats or an archived one.

- Confirmation before marking all chats as read. The command now asks first, with No as the default, so a stray Alt followed by Enter no longer marks everything as read, on your phone too. The question has a "Don't show again" option: ticked and confirmed with Yes, later requests mark everything straight away. To be asked again, use the "Ask for confirmation before marking all chats as read" option in the User Interface tab of Settings.

- Profile backup in Settings. The new Profile backup tab sets how often WinZapp renews, when it closes, the copy of the browser profile it uses to recover the connection, every 24 hours by default. The backup can also be taken while WinZapp is open, at the interval you choose: WhatsApp is disconnected while it runs, which can take a few minutes, and WinZapp can ask before each one. A new backup only replaces the previous one once WhatsApp has connected again with it. If you would rather not spend the space on the backup, tick "Don't keep profile backups", knowing that if the profile is damaged you will need to pair again; when you tick it, WinZapp says how much space the backups take and asks whether to delete them.

- Export and import your settings. In the File menu, “Export settings” writes your preferences to a file and “Import settings” applies that file here, right away, without restarting — for moving everything to another computer or another account. Your WhatsApp connection, and what belongs to one account such as cleared chats and per-chat sounds, stay out of the file. If you run your own API, its address, port and key travel with it.

- Space plays and pauses the focused audio or video. That key did nothing in the message list before. It stays narrower than Enter: it never opens a link, a document, the viewer or a contact's conversation.

- Selection mode. Once you have selected the first item with Ctrl+Space, Space on its own selects and deselects the following ones — in the chat list, in the message list and in the forward window — without holding Ctrl for every one of them. The screen reader says when the mode turns on and when it turns off, and it turns off by itself as soon as nothing is selected any more. If you would rather Space always played, untick "Use Space to select/deselect whenever a selection already exists" in Settings, User Interface.

- Esc deselects before closing. With messages selected, the first Esc only deselects them all and just the second one closes the conversation. It can be turned off in Settings, User Interface too.

- Stereo voice messages. With a microphone that really records in stereo, a voice message can keep the sound of both sides. Tick "Record voice messages in stereo by default" in the Audio Devices tab of Settings, or use the new button below "Record voice message" (Ctrl+Shift+G) to record just one message in the other mode: stereo when the default is mono, mono when it is stereo. A stereo recording is sent as an audio message rather than a voice message, so it plays on iPhone too. If the microphone doesn't provide two channels, the message is recorded in mono and WinZapp tells you.

- Resync only the open chat with Shift+F5, also in the Sync menu. Unlike F5, which deletes and downloads every chat again, Shift+F5 fetches the open chat's messages from WhatsApp again, puts any missing ones in the right place and removes the ones that no longer exist there, without touching older history. F5 and Shift+F5 now ask for confirmation, with No as the default and a "Don't show again" option; to be asked again, use the matching options in the User Interface tab of Settings. When the chat is already up to date, or WhatsApp has no messages in it, Shift+F5 says so instead of saying it couldn't resync.

- The global hotkey that brings WinZapp back from the system tray now also sends it there: with WinZapp in the foreground, the same hotkey hides it to the tray. In every other situation it still brings the window to the front.

- Record the microphone together with computer audio. The new "Record microphone and computer audio" button (Ctrl+Shift+H) records your voice and whatever the computer is playing, such as a video, some music or the screen reader itself, into a single message. While recording, two sliders set the volume of other computer sounds and of NVDA separately; without NVDA, the second one is disabled. These volumes only affect the recording, not what you hear, and are remembered per account. Before it starts, WinZapp warns that everything playing on the computer goes into the recording, with a "Don't show again" option; to be warned again, use "Warn before recording computer audio" in the User Interface tab. The recording is sent as audio.

- Close just the current account. With more than one account, "Close current account" in the Accounts menu (Ctrl+F4) closes only the account you are in, freeing its memory and connection, and moves you to another open account; the others keep running. To open it again, pick it from another account's Accounts menu or press Ctrl+Alt with its number. If it is the only open account, WinZapp asks first, since closing it quits the program.

- Quickly switch the audio device. Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H opens the list of playback devices and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+G the list of recording devices, numbered 1 to 0: press the number to switch at once, or use the arrows and Enter. The switch applies to sounds, voice messages and calls — a call in progress moves to the new device without dropping — and to every open account, until you close WinZapp. The devices chosen in Settings stay as they are.

- Choose what background notifications say. In the General tab of Settings, "Background notification content" offers three levels: sound, chat or group name and message text, as before; sound and chat or group name, without the text; or sound only, with no notification on screen and nothing read out. Notifications for locked chats still show neither name nor content.

- Return to the search results when closing a chat. With "Keep the chat search when opening a chat" ticked in the User Interface tab of Settings, the chat search stays active when you open a chat it found, and Esc puts the focus back on that chat among the results instead of on the full list. The option is off by default.

- Forward voice messages as voice messages. WhatsApp turns every forwarded voice message into a plain audio, which takes it out of the sequential playback of voice messages in the destination chat. With "Forward voice messages as voice messages instead of audio" ticked in the User Interface tab of Settings, the copy arrives as a voice message and is still marked as forwarded. The option is off by default.

- Download updates in the background. With "Download updates in the background, without the progress window" ticked in the General tab of Settings, an update you accepted, of WinZapp or of WPPConnect, downloads without the progress window, and you keep using the app normally, connected. Only when it is ready does WinZapp tell you it is about to install: for an update of WinZapp itself it closes and opens again; for one of WPPConnect it goes offline only for the moment it takes to switch versions and restart it, instead of for the whole download and install. If you are on a call, the notice waits for it to end. The option applies to every account and is off by default.

IMPROVEMENTS

- Spell checking: the error sound now also plays when you type punctuation (comma, full stop, question mark and so on) right after a misspelled word, not only a space, and when the cursor arrives at a misspelled word with the arrow keys, Ctrl+arrows, Home, End or the mouse.

- A major reorganization of the code. WinZapp's two largest files, which on their own added up to more than 50,000 lines and held almost everything the program does, were split into dozens of smaller parts, each taking care of one thing: sync, connection, sending messages, calls, the chat list and so on. Nothing changes for you as a user. For developers it becomes much easier to find, understand and fix each part, and much harder for a fix to break something else unnoticed — which is what keeps WinZapp healthy in the long run.

- WinZapp's logs and the local server's (log.log and wppconnect.log) no longer contain phone numbers, WhatsApp identifiers or contact names: they appear masked. You can now send your logs when reporting a problem without exposing your contacts.

- The Polish translation was revised.

- Audio files pasted into the message field with Ctrl+V can now be sent as audio or as a document, with the new "Audio files pasted into the message field (Ctrl+V)" option in the Files and saving tab of Settings; the default is audio. The message shows up in the chat with the type it will be sent as right away.

- Archived and locked chats can now be selected just like the main conversations: Ctrl+Space, Shift+arrows, Shift+Home/End, Ctrl+Shift+Space and selection-mode Space, with the same sound and announcements. The "Bulk actions" menu (with its own shortcuts) can unarchive, clear, delete and mark archived chats as read or unread, and unlock or mark locked chats as read or unread.

- Automatic media download now follows the "Automatically download media" option in the Storage tab of Settings right away: unchecking it stops the download in progress, and checking it (or checking another media type) starts downloading without waiting for the next sync. When unchecked, it now also applies to media in messages that arrive while WinZapp is open, and the list of types is disabled, keeping what was checked.

- WinZapp now keeps its list of WhatsApp Web versions up to date. When it starts, it downloads a newer one in the background if there is one and applies it the next time WinZapp starts, which fixes calls failing on installs where that list had fallen behind.

- When a call cannot start because the WhatsApp Web version in use does not support calls, WinZapp now says so and what to try (restart WinZapp, or choose "Force reinstall WPPConnect" in the Help menu) instead of reading out an error code.

- The installer is now much smaller to download: WinZapp's files inside it are compressed, as they already were in the portable ZIP, and the installer checks each file while installing.

- The installer and the uninstaller now appear in every language WinZapp is available in: Brazilian Portuguese, European Portuguese, Spanish, Polish, Romanian, Turkish and English. They follow the Windows display language and show English for any other.

- The WhatsApp library WinZapp uses was updated. Its developers' notes list fixes for creating groups, getting and resetting group invite links, editing group descriptions, changing your profile name and uploading media on recent WhatsApp Web versions. If WinZapp offers to update WPPConnect when it starts, accept it so the new library is installed.

- Conversation search with F3 and Shift+F3. You can now jump to the next result with F3 and the previous one with Shift+F3 from anywhere in the open conversation, including the messages list, without going back to the search field; focus follows the result and the screen reader announces its position. Enter and Shift+Enter still work as an alternative, but only inside the search field, because in the messages list they already do other things. The "Previous result" and "Next result" buttons now announce Shift+F3 and F3, and the shortcuts help was updated.

- Forwarding now confirms when it worked. Before, only a failure was announced; now a box appears at the end saying "Message forwarded." or how many messages were forwarded.

- Chats you lock with WhatsApp Chat Lock on your phone are now hidden in WinZapp too. They leave the conversation list and appear only under "Locked chats" (Alt+7), after your WinZapp PIN; if you never set one up, WinZapp offers to create it the first time. Only your phone can unlock them, so WinZapp does not offer "Unlock chat" for those chats, and a chat you unlock on the phone returns to the list on its own.

FIXES

- Calls no longer fail with a "NumPy was built with baseline optimizations" error, followed by "cannot load module more than once per process", on computers whose processor is older or limited by a virtual machine. WinZapp now ships a NumPy build that runs on any 64-bit processor.

- Calls: fixed the "cannot load module more than once per process" error that could appear when starting a voice or video call. WhatsApp's call engine is now started only once and shared, instead of being started again by each call action.

- When you messaged someone who isn't in your contacts and has no name on their WhatsApp profile, the conversation started showing your own name instead of their phone number. It now keeps showing the phone number until their real name is found.

- The uninstaller now follows the Windows display language like the installer does, instead of always appearing in Portuguese.

- Forwarding messages no longer fails on recent WhatsApp Web versions, where every forward was refused with the error “forwardMessages function is not available”.

- Starting a voice message with recording announcements silenced no longer lets the screen reader begin saying “Send” before being cut off. WinZapp now avoids the automatic focus move that caused the clipped announcement while keeping Ctrl+R, Ctrl+Shift+P and Ctrl+Shift+D available.

- Messages you had selected in a conversation stayed selected after you closed it, and came back selected when you opened it again. The selection now ends with the conversation.

- When WhatsApp refused the connection right after the app was opened, the backup copy of the browser profile was never restored: if pairing codes started arriving in the first few seconds, WinZapp closed the session and sent you to pair again even with a good copy saved. It now restores the copy as soon as the first code appears, and only asks you to pair when the restore does not help.

- Adding a group member by phone number did nothing: the "Add number" button added nobody and showed no error. It now adds the number right away and tells you whether it worked, and an invalid number is reported. In the same dialog, the search field is now read as "Search contact".

- With a chat open, the message list shrank back to the configured limit every minute, and older messages disappeared — including from the stored history. WinZapp mistook messages outside the range it checks on WhatsApp for messages deleted on the phone. A message is now only removed when WhatsApp shows it really no longer exists — never just because it could not be found, and never within the period a profile recovery rolled back — and the list no longer shrinks while you read.

- In groups with many participants, group info could sit on "Loading..." for minutes — Overview and the Participants list only appeared after WinZapp had looked up, one at a time, the number of every member whose number it did not know yet. It looked frozen, and only closing and reopening the dialog brought it back. The info now appears as soon as the group answers, and missing names and numbers are filled into the list afterwards, without moving you off the row you are on.

- Changing the language in Settings and pressing Apply now translates every option. Some stayed in the previous language until Settings was closed and opened again, such as "When opening a chat, show up to this many messages" and "Messages to skip when pressing Page Up / Page Down".

- The default audio playback speed no longer drops back to 1x on its own. When Settings opened, the field in the Audio playback tab was empty whatever speed was saved, and pressing Apply and then OK saved 1x over it. The saved speed is now shown selected and kept.

- After WinZapp recovers the browser profile, chats with unread messages no longer show up as read.

- Copying part of the message text window (Alt+C) brought along line breaks the message did not have, wherever the window splits long lines to make them easier to read. Copying now gives you the message as it was written, with only its own line breaks.

- Messages WhatsApp had not managed to load yet disappeared from the chat, and a reply to one of them quoted a message that appeared nowhere. This happens, for example, with messages from someone who changed phones, until that person's phone sends them again. They now show as "Waiting for this message. This may take a while.", as in WhatsApp, and are replaced by the text as soon as it arrives, or earlier, when someone replies quoting the message.

- A group's unread counter could disappear when someone reacted to a message, even with messages still unread, and in groups with system notices it went down on its own every minute. The counter now shows the messages that really are unread.

- When copying a message (Ctrl+C), opening its text (Alt+C) or copying several at once, the people it mentioned came out as phone numbers, or as a meaningless string of digits, instead of the name shown in the message list. They now come out as names.

- With a custom notification sound and no file chosen, in a chat's data or in the Alert Tones tab of Settings, the preview button stayed available and showed an error. It now only appears when the typed path leads to a file that exists.

- With more than one account open, updating never finished: WinZapp reopened on the old version and offered the same update again, without explaining what had happened. It now closes the other accounts before installing, and if an update cannot be completed, it tells you the next time it opens.

- Messages to Meta AI were not delivered, and its replies showed up empty. WhatsApp requires Meta AI's terms of service to be accepted before the first message: WinZapp now shows those terms, with a link to read them, and records your acceptance. If a message is still refused, the notice explains why, and Meta AI's replies show their text.

- In a reply, the links of the quoted message were treated as the reply's own links: they became a Tab stop, could form the links list together with the reply's own links, and Enter opened the quoted link instead of showing the message text. Only the reply's own links count now.

- Clicking a message notification while the Status panel was open opened the chat behind it and left keyboard focus stuck on an invisible control until you pressed Alt+1. The same happened when opening a participant's chat from a group's info. WinZapp now shows the chat list before opening the chat.

- In a group's info, Enter on a participant you had never talked to, or who only showed as a number, closed the dialog and left you in the group. It now opens the private chat with them.

- After adding a local contact from a chat's info, the message list kept showing the name the person uses on WhatsApp until WinZapp was restarted. The new name now shows right away.

- When pairing a different number from the previous one, WinZapp deletes the old account's data, but a sync that was already running kept filling the list with that account's chats for a few minutes, which then disappeared again. That sync now stops at once.

- After the audio output device changed — a USB or Bluetooth headset unplugged, the Windows default device switched, or the output changed in Settings — the audio message you asked to play could simply not play, with no warning, and only worked on the second try. WinZapp now moves to the default device and plays the message the first time.

- When the computer woke from sleep with no internet yet, WinZapp could stay disconnected even after the internet came back, until it was closed and opened again. It now waits for the network before reopening the connection to WhatsApp.

- When several messages from the same chat arrived together, right after you had read that chat in WinZapp, the unread counter could show fewer messages than arrived: three files sent at once showed as two unread. The counter now shows them all.

- Leaving a chat open and switching to archived chats (Alt+4) made WinZapp keep treating that chat as the one you are reading: its new messages were marked as read, played the open-chat sound and were spoken aloud, even though you were in another panel. That now only happens while the chat's panel is on screen; the chat stays open, and you find it where you left it when you come back.

- In archived chats, Alt+M did not move to the messages list of the open chat, as it does in the main chats. It now works from anywhere in the panel.

- A chat you have open in one list is now hidden when you switch to another: the main chats (Alt+1), the archived chats (Alt+4) or the locked chats. Switching only changes the list and puts you on it; it does not show or reload the open chat. The chat stays open, and you bring it back with Alt+M, Alt+2 or Alt+3, or by opening it again; it then appears in the list it was opened from. This also fixes an archived chat staying on screen in the main chats. A chat opened from the main chats belongs to them, even an archived one found with the search field.

- The same goes for any other panel, such as Status or Calls: with a chat left open behind it, its new messages are announced like those of any other chat and are not marked as read. Messages from archived chats are only announced while the archived list is on screen.

- The “is typing” and “is recording audio” announcements kept being spoken for a chat left open behind another panel (Alt+4, Status, Calls...). They are now announced only while that chat is on screen, as marking as read and the sounds already were.

- In a chat that had ever been cleared, the unread counter disappeared on its own about a minute after a message arrived, and opening the chat didn't mark it as read on your phone. It happened mostly in busy, muted groups. Now the counter stays until you read the chat, and reading it in WinZapp marks it as read on your phone too.

- View once messages arrived with no counter, sound or notification, and after a sync they stayed forever as a message still waiting to load. Now it shows "View once message. Open it on your phone to see it.", with the sound and notification of a new message. The content itself still stays on the phone, as in WhatsApp Web.

- Opened from a network folder — for example, the Downloads folder of a Parallels virtual machine on a Mac, which actually lives on the other computer — WinZapp couldn't install its local server and showed an unintelligible error. Now it says it can't run from there and explains how to fix it: install with WinZappInstaller, or move the folder to a drive on the computer itself.

- Answering the update prompt while WinZapp was still starting could leave it without a window, running in the background, and it only opened again after the process was ended or the computer restarted. The prompt now waits for the main window to open, and if a shutdown gets stuck WinZapp ends itself instead of staying stuck.

- The screen reader no longer re-reads the focused message in the open conversation for no reason. When a new message arrived together with a reaction (or after a message you sent), the automatic refresh rewrote the whole message list and NVDA repeated the item you were on; now the list is updated row by row, and only the rows that really changed are touched.

- The "typing" and "recording audio" announcements are spoken in groups again. WhatsApp started reporting who is typing in a way WinZapp did not read, so only one-to-one chats announced it.

- Choosing a conversation that was already open from the list now respects the "When opening a chat, focus" setting (message field, or unread separator / last message); it always went to the message field before.

- Replying to a message while mentioning someone in the reply sent it to the recipients as a new message, without the quote, although WinZapp showed it as a reply. The quote now goes along with the mention.

- Update notices now also appear when WinZapp starts with Windows: neither WinZapp's own update nor the WPPConnect Server one was ever offered in that case. The WPPConnect Server notice also now offers a newer release than the one WinZapp was tested with, as a recommended update you can decline, instead of only when WinZapp itself raised the required version.

- In a chat whose older messages are only on your phone, WinZapp kept asking the phone for them when you reached the first message: nothing arrived, the phone could show a sync notification each time, and the list stayed busy for minutes. WinZapp now detects this case, asks nothing and announces "Use WhatsApp on your phone to see older messages."

V1.1.1.0

This release is about keeping your account connected. WinZapp now keeps a backup copy of the browser profile — which is where your WhatsApp connection actually lives — and restores it on its own when it stops being accepted, telling you what happened. Alongside it come fixes for the situations that broke the session and left the app asking you to pair again: shutting the computer down, suspending and resuming, and the app restarting the connection by itself. Spell checking in the message field and link actions arrived too.

WHAT'S NEW

- Automatic browser profile recovery. Your WhatsApp connection is stored in the browser profile WinZapp uses underneath, and when that profile stopped being accepted the only way out was to pair again. WinZapp now keeps a backup copy of it, keeps the previous copy as well, and restores it on its own when it sees the connection was refused — saying out loud that it restored and is reconnecting. It never offers back a copy WhatsApp has already refused, and when no usable copy exists it says so plainly instead of leaving you offline with no explanation.

- Spell checking in the message field. In Settings you choose between following Windows' own spelling setting (the default), always on, or always off. There is also a dedicated spelling-error sound, configurable alongside the other sounds.

- Link actions on messages. When a message contains links, the context menu now offers Open link and Copy link for the link you are on, and Ctrl+C copies that link instead of the whole message.

- A warning when the internal browser is incomplete. If the browser WinZapp uses to connect to WhatsApp cannot start — usually because antivirus software removed part of it — the app says so in plain words and explains what to do, instead of looking like a WhatsApp problem.

- A warning for an unconfirmed send. When WhatsApp accepts a message but does not confirm delivery, WinZapp tells you to check the conversation before resending, rather than risking the same message arriving twice.

IMPROVEMENTS

- Starting with Windows now connects far sooner. The app used to wait for the connection only after it had already tried to use it, and spent a long time showing "disconnected from WhatsApp" for no reason. It now waits for everything to be ready before appearing in the system tray.

- The message list is no longer rebuilt in full every minute. Only the rows that actually changed are rewritten, so the screen reader stops re-reading the message you are on and the selection sound no longer fires by itself.

- Fewer sync notifications on your phone. WinZapp only asks the phone for older history in conversations you have opened, one request at a time, and stops asking altogether once a conversation has answered that it has nothing more.

- Conversations the sync had stopped looking at are checked again on their own, without you having to refresh with F5.

- With several accounts open at once, a WinZapp update opens a single window instead of one per account.

- Audio: WinZapp asks the microphone for its own sample rate before trying the fixed ones, and sound output recovers on its own when the default device changes or is unplugged.

- Reactions that arrived while a conversation was closed now show up when you open it.

- Line breaks in the message box can now be navigated normally by the screen reader.

- Updated WhatsApp connection (WPPConnect 2.10.21), including security fixes, and WinZapp now notices on its own when that connection has drifted and needs reinstalling.

FIXES

- Shutting the computer down no longer breaks the session. WinZapp closed the WhatsApp connection too late in the Windows shutdown, once the process holding it had already been killed — and the profile came back unusable, asking to pair again next time. It now closes everything while Windows is still asking whether it may shut down.

- Suspending the computer no longer breaks the session. On resume, WinZapp restarted the connection without waiting for the browser to finish writing what it had in hand, and that alone was enough to lose the pairing. It now waits.

- Replying to a status works again. The reply failed saying it could not be sent.

- An ambiguous send failure no longer produces duplicate messages: when there is no way to tell whether WhatsApp received it, WinZapp does not resend on its own.

- Downloading large documents is possible again. Files of a few hundred megabytes announced "downloading" and produced nothing.

- The upload and download progress indicator now reports the transfer that is actually happening, and no longer keeps spinning on a message that has finished or appears on the wrong row.

- Cancelling or closing the pairing screen no longer deletes your conversations, and neither does a momentary authentication failure at startup. History is only cleared when a different phone number is genuinely connected — and WinZapp tells you when that happens.

- The QR code is only announced once it is genuinely on screen. WinZapp used to say "the QR code was updated" before any code had been shown at all, which made the code seem to appear only on the second try. The pairing screen now says the code is being generated, announces it when it actually appears, and says when it could not be generated or when the pairing code has expired — instead of staying silent or announcing an update that never happened.

- Unread counts: conversations no longer come back as unread without a new message having arrived.

- The phantom character that appeared in the message field when returning from another program with NVDA running is gone.

- Copying a voice message now works.

- Conversations that showed a number instead of a name are now resolved.

- Switching accounts now clears the previous account's media properly, which could otherwise be left behind on the computer.

V1.1.0.0

This release adds the Media tab, a media viewer in its own window, link previews, bulk actions and an Accessibility tab in settings. Conversation syncing is much faster, and the conversation list is no longer rebuilt on every message, which cuts down a great deal of screen-reader re-reading.

WHAT'S NEW

- Media tab in conversations and groups. It lists photos, videos, audio files, voice messages, documents and links, with filtering by type and by downloaded or not downloaded, full history loading, and open, save and delete actions on each message. Audio files and voice messages are two separate filters: you can show only one or only the other, and choose whether automatic downloading fetches one, the other or both.

- Media viewer in its own window, with play and pause, skip forward and back 10 seconds, position, volume and speed. You choose in settings whether conversation videos and statuses open in it.

- Link previews in messages. When a message has two or more links, they become a list you can navigate. Your outgoing messages now carry a preview too.

- Bulk actions on messages and conversations: copy, star, pin, forward, save and delete messages; mark conversations read or unread, archive and clear them. Each action has its own shortcut, and Ctrl+C copies the selection.

- Accessibility tab in settings, with extended screen-reader compatibility and the option to use the SAPI voice when no screen reader is running.

- Files and saving tab, to choose whether save dialogs open in the last folder you used, in your Downloads folder, or in a folder you pick.

- Reactions: you can now see which reaction is yours and remove it by selecting it again.

- Save a received contact message as a contact, with Enter or Space.

- Play back the audio you have already recorded while recording is paused, with Ctrl+P.

- Rebuilt country selector in the pairing dialog: it uses the country and language configured in Windows, is sorted alphabetically, accepts multi-letter type-ahead search, and protects the country code from accidental editing.

- New settings: show "yesterday" instead of the date, announce "Forwarded" before the sender, tell voice messages apart from audio files, which is now the default instead of reading everything as audio, determine a video's length as soon as it is downloaded, choose which media types are shown by default, and stop marking audio as played.

- Search in the contact lists ("Attach contact" and "Add member"): the field is focused as soon as the list opens, filters by name (including surname) and by number as you type, and clearing it shows every contact again.

- Received contact card: a context menu with view name and number, copy the number with Ctrl+C, and start a chat. When the card carries more than one number, WinZapp asks which one to use.

IMPROVEMENTS

- Documents can now be up to 2 GB, which is WhatsApp's real limit. WinZapp used to stop them at 1 GB for no reason. Photos, videos and audio stay at 1 GB, which is WhatsApp's limit for those.

- Incremental conversation syncing. Starting the app and refreshing is much faster, and WinZapp announces when the update starts and when it finishes.

- The conversation list is far faster. A new message or a delivery receipt updates only that one row instead of rebuilding the whole list. In practice, the screen reader re-reads far less.

- Actions on a message also update only that message's row, and editing a message no longer freezes the interface.

- Notifications respect Windows Focus Assist.

- Groups: a notice when only admins may send messages, with the message field set to read-only. The add-member dialog shows names instead of numbers and reports who could not be added.

- Attaching a contact: the list has no duplicates, is sorted, and no longer shows unresolved entries.

- Audio: OGG files the app could not decode are now converted automatically, and sending WAV files works.

FIXES

- Session dropped by WhatsApp: WinZapp now says so straight away and opens pairing, instead of leaving you on the conversation list while repeating that the QR code has been updated. With nobody on the pairing screen the session is closed rather than left asking WhatsApp for a new code forever — that endless stream of requests could get the account banned — and WinZapp says out loud that it closed the session and that you need to connect again.

- Unread counts: already-read messages no longer come back as new, a chat read on your phone is respected, and the open conversation no longer loses its count.

- The unread separator and your reading position are no longer lost while you read. The open conversation was being rebuilt periodically, which interrupted the screen reader mid-sentence.

- Session: closing and reopening WinZapp no longer asks you to pair again. Shutdown now waits for the session to be written before closing.

- Pairing: when WhatsApp refuses the code, the real reason is shown, and a server update no longer interrupts pairing in progress.

- Automatic updates work again when the app is installed in a folder with accents or other special characters.

- Privacy: the app no longer writes message content or credentials to the log file.

- Sending: cancelling a message that had already been sent is now explained clearly, and large uploads are no longer lost along the way.

- Videos with no reported length no longer show "0 seconds".

- The first message from a contact no longer disappears when the conversation is opened.

- Accessibility: the emoji shortcut is now reported to the screen reader, Alt+T works inside a conversation again, and speech is no longer cut off by every delivery receipt.

- Syncing no longer restarts over and over. A single chat WhatsApp does not recognise, and that was never a real conversation, was enough to make WinZapp believe the sync had never finished: it started again from scratch every few minutes, announcing itself each time, even with everything already loaded.

- The fast conversation refresh no longer skips chats that received new messages. They stayed on their old contents until you pressed F5 and synced everything, which defeated the point of the faster refresh.

- WinZapp now knows the expiry date of the WhatsApp Web version it uses. When it lapses, it fetches the one WhatsApp is currently serving instead of insisting on one that is no longer accepted. This is what stalled syncing, left the conversation list empty, and ended with WhatsApp dropping the computer from your linked devices.

- The first pairing code after a dropped connection no longer fails. WinZapp was requesting codes on its own, in the background, until WhatsApp refused for too many requests; it now waits between requests, and when WhatsApp does refuse, the message explains why instead of showing an internal error.

- The "not downloaded" media filter was listing voice messages that were already on the computer, including ones you had played inside the app.

- A document you send now shows its file size as soon as it appears in the message list, like received ones already did.

- WinZapp now prefers a stable WhatsApp Web build over an alpha one when both are available — using an alpha was getting the device unpaired hours after pairing. The initial sync is also more tolerant of slowness before it decides the connection is down.

- The "Messages to myself" chat no longer shows delivery and read receipts, or the "played" audio mark — they make no sense in a chat with yourself.

- "Delete for everyone" in the "Messages to myself" chat now actually works. The message used to disappear only locally and come back on the next sync.

- Alt+2, Alt+3 and Ctrl+W with no conversation open now say "No chat is open" instead of doing nothing.

- Alt+2 in an empty conversation says "This chat is empty" instead of staying silent.

- The conversation list returns to the row focus was actually on, not always to the conversation that had been open.

- The unread separator no longer disappears or shows the wrong count when the app is minimized to the system tray with the conversation open.

- An archived conversation is no longer announced out loud when a new message arrives while the app is in the foreground.

- Playing voice messages back to back no longer reads the whole previous row out loud over the new one.

- The option to silence the screen reader while recording audio now stops the announcement at the source, instead of trying to cancel it after it has already started.

- Adding a new contact through WinZapp could duplicate the conversation and leave its first message permanently stuck as "not confirmed" whenever WhatsApp recognized that number in a different format (e.g. with or without the 9th digit).

- A group's name changed on WhatsApp is now picked up by WinZapp, including on an F5 resync. Before, the old name was never replaced.

- Switching the sound output device in Settings while an audio was playing could make the first attempt to play another audio silently fail; it now plays on the first try.

- Disconnecting from File > Disconnect no longer plays the "connected"/"sync started" sounds again or tries to reconnect on its own — the account stays properly disconnected.

- The update installer's log now lives inside the account's own logs folder (alongside log.log and shutdown_audit.log), instead of loose in the program folder.
