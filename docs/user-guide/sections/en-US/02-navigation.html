The panels are also in a navigation list (Alt+M takes you to it), in the same order: Communities comes after Calls alpha 4397, with Alt+8 in the item's name, and you can reach it by moving down the list.

If no chat is open, Alt+2, Alt+3 and Ctrl+W say "No chat is open"; in an empty chat, Alt+2 says "This chat is empty".

The open chat stays "behind" the panels

When you switch panels (Status, Calls, archived...), the open chat stays open, just hidden. While it is hidden, its new messages are announced as coming from another chat and are not marked as read, and "typing" notices are not spoken. Go back to it with Alt+M, Alt+2 or Alt+3, or by opening it again.

Search all conversations: Ctrl+Shift+G, or "Search all conversations" in the File menu, opens a window that searches the text of every chat stored on this computer at once. Type what to look for and press Enter; the results list reads "chat — sender — date — excerpt", always by name. Every word you type has to appear, in any order and with the same handling of case and accents as the chat search; the search covers message text, captions, file names, polls, contact cards, places, the transcriptions and AI descriptions saved with voice messages, photos and videos, and the names of the sender and of the chat, but at least one word must be in the message itself, so a chat's name alone does not list all its messages. Enter on a result, or "Open in the conversation", opens that chat on that message. The history is encrypted and is read in the background, a page at a time: the window never freezes, Esc stops a search that is running (a second Esc closes the window), and at most 200 results are listed, newest stored first. The screen reader says only "Searching..." and then the number of results. Locked chats are left out while the vault is closed, with no hint of how many; with the vault open they are searched like any other. Nothing you search for is written to the log.

System tray and global shortcut

WinZapp can stay in the system tray and start with Windows. There is a global shortcut (configurable in Settings > Interface > Keyboard shortcuts) that brings the window to the front from any program; with WinZapp already in the foreground, the same shortcut hides it in the tray. When started with Windows, it only shows up in the tray once the connection is ready.

Spoken announcements

Everything WinZapp says goes through your screen reader (or through the Windows SAPI voice, if you enable that option in Settings > Accessibility and no screen reader is active). In Settings > Accessibility > Spoken content you choose what is announced.

Contacts panel

Contacts: the navigation list gains "Contacts" after Channels, with no Alt+number shortcut. It shows the saved contacts, one row per person by name and number; a contact kept under two addresses (its number and its linked ID, or a Brazilian number with and without the 9th digit) is a single row. "Search contacts" filters by name or number. Buttons and the context menu (Applications key or Shift+F10) open the conversation, start a voice or video call, show "Contact details", delete the contact, or block or unblock it; "New contact" adds one. "Enter on a contact" chooses what Enter does: open the conversation, call, video call or show the details.
