Every new alpha that brings features is added to the guide. This list shows up to which alpha the guide is complete and what was added in each round.

AlphaWhat went into the guide
Not yet releasedVideos: in the AI window of a video, the new "Understand full video" button tells what is seen and what is said in one text. It uses the transcription already saved with the video, or transcribes the speech on this computer (a progress window with Cancel); the sound never leaves the computer. A consent of its own, asked every time the window opens and never remembered, says that the pictures taken from the video and the transcribed text are sent, and names the providers. If the speech cannot be transcribed you are told why and may describe only the images, and the answer then says it covers only the images. The text sent is limited to its first 12,000 characters. Follow-up questions and "Run again (new request)" keep working; the answer is not put in the message line, so it never replaces the saved description or transcription.
Not yet releasedSettings, Online AI: the new "Providers by task..." button opens a window where each kind of task (voice messages, photos, stickers, videos, PDF, message summary and writing help) can have its own providers in its own order. Check "Use the general order for this task" to keep things as they are; otherwise use "Include or leave out" and the move buttons to build the route. A task with its own route only uses the providers in it (if none is ready, the action is not offered), and every provider still asks for your consent as before.
Not yet releasedChat summary: a new opt-in option, "Summarize older messages too (several requests)" (Settings, Online AI, "Types of media handled..."), lets a long backlog be summarized in up to 6 parts plus one request that joins them, instead of dropping the older messages. The consent says how many requests may be made, the window says "Summarizing part 2 of 5...", and if a part fails no summary is shown as if it were complete.
Not yet releasedLocal AI models: Settings, Online AI now lists Ollama and LM Studio next to the online providers. You give the server's address (the usual one is filled in) and choose a model you installed; WinZapp then describes pictures, stickers and video frames, summarizes chats, helps you write and runs the quick analysis modes on your own computer, with no API key and nothing sent out. Without a chosen model the provider is not used, and the window says so. If you set an address that is not on this computer, WinZapp treats it as an online provider and asks for your consent first. Voice messages and PDFs are not handled by local models.
Not yet releasedAI answers: a new option in Settings, Online AI, "Types of media handled...", "Show answers as they arrive" (off by default), makes the result window of a description, a PDF or a chat summary fill in whole lines or sentences while the AI is still writing, at most once a second, instead of waiting for the complete answer. The waiting sound stops and the window says once that the answer is arriving; the answer is still read aloud once, when it is complete. If the request fails or you cancel it, the partial text is discarded, and Cancel and Esc close the connection at once. Voice messages and the writing help are not affected.
Not yet releasedA video can now keep an AI description and a local transcription at the same time. When both are put in the message's line, the line says both, for example "..., description: ..., transcription: ...". Deleting the transcription (Alt+Shift+Delete or the context menu) keeps the description; deleting again removes the description. A message deleted for everyone loses both.
Not yet releasedWriting help: besides the four actions, "Use my own instruction" lets you type what to do with the draft (for example, "summarize in one line"), and "Suggest a reply draft" asks the AI for a reply based on the last 10 messages of the conversation and your draft. This sends those messages and the names of who wrote them, so it asks for its own consent, every time in locked chats. The suggestion only replaces your draft if you choose "Replace draft".

It is refused with a clear sentence while you record a voice message or are in a call. The key can be changed in Settings > Keyboard shortcuts.
Not yet releasedLocal transcription vocabulary: in Settings, Local transcription, Advanced options, "Vocabulary" takes names, acronyms and terms (one per line) that Whisper should expect, to get them spelled right. The list stays on this computer, is never written to the log and never goes online; whisper.cpp only uses the terms without accents. A transcription already saved is not redone when the list changes.
