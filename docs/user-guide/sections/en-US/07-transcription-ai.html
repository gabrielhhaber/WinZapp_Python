There are two different paths, with different commands: local transcription (Whisper, on your computer, nothing leaves it) and online AI (sends the media to the provider you configure).

Local transcription with Whisper

Turns voice messages, audio and videos into text on your own computer. Everything is configured in the Local Transcription tab of Settings.

ShortcutAction
Alt+Shift+TTranscribes the message and opens the text in a window (copy, save or insert into the message); if a saved transcription exists, shows it
Alt+Shift+NPuts the transcription on the message's row ("..., duration: 0:12, transcription: ...") and reads it aloud, with no window
Alt+Shift+DeleteDeletes the message's saved transcription
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+TTranscribes the selected messages and shows them together
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+NTranscribes the selected ones on their own rows
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+DeleteDeletes the saved transcriptions of the selected ones

In the context menu: "Transcribe message locally with Whisper", "View transcription", "Transcribe again" and "Delete transcription".

  • Components: faster-whisper is the default and comes with WinZapp; whisper.cpp is optional and experimental, and the tab installs it in the version for the processor or for NVIDIA graphics cards. faster-whisper also uses an NVIDIA card once you install the "CUDA libraries" in the same tab.
  • Models: they are only downloaded when you ask, from tiny to large-v3 and large-v3-turbo; in whisper.cpp there are also smaller versions (8 and 5 bits) and some 32-bit ones. Some models understand only one language (marked in the list). In the list, Ctrl+Space marks models and "Download" downloads all the marked ones, one after the other. "Where to run the transcription" and the "Calculation precision: {precision}" stay on Automatic or can be chosen.
  • Reuse what you already have: "Models in other folders" uses models downloaded elsewhere without copying them, and "Reuse downloads from another folder" points to the CUDA libraries and to the whisper.cpp programs already downloaded. The models folder can be moved, with the models along.
  • Memory: "Keep the model loaded in memory (faster-whisper and whisper.cpp)" avoids reloading the model for each message. Turned off, the model is released 5 minutes after the last transcription.
  • Per model: the text stored in each message is per model. When you switch models, the message is transcribed again and the previous model's text stays stored. "Clear this model's saved transcriptions" and "Clear saved transcriptions of all models" clean the cache. The cache can be turned on or off in general and per model.
  • No progress window: the option "Transcribe without the progress window: stay on the messages or statuses until the text is ready (the same shortcut cancels)" makes Alt+Shift+T and Alt+Shift+N transcribe in the background; the same shortcut on the same message cancels.
  • Automatic: "Automatically transcribe received voice messages and videos and put the text in the message line" transcribes each new voice message and video (up to 10 minutes of audio, one at a time, outside locked chats), with no windows or per-message announcements. If transcription is not ready, WinZapp pauses and says why just once.
  • Enter and Space: with "Enter and Space on a voice message or video transcribe it into its line in the list instead of playing it" (Settings > Audio), Enter and Space transcribe and Alt+Shift+N plays or pauses. Turned off, Enter and Space play and Alt+Shift+N transcribes on the row.
  • Videos: the video's sound is converted and transcribed, with the same options. GIFs are ignored and a video without sound is reported as such.
  • In bulk: WinZapp says how many messages will be transcribed, skipped or reused, transcribes one at a time ("message 3 of 12"), continues if one fails and says at the end how many succeeded, were skipped and failed. The same shortcut or the context menu cancels the queue, and the context menu shows all the transcriptions together, with sender, date and time, to copy or save. With "Replace shortcuts with bulk actions when selecting chats and messages", Alt+Shift+T and Alt+Shift+N also act on the selection.
  • Background downloads: with "Download updates and transcription downloads in the background, without the progress window" (Settings > General), models, whisper.cpp and CUDA download without the progress window, and "Cancel download in the background" stops it.
  • Status: audio and video statuses can be transcribed in the Status tab, with the same keys (see the Status section).
  • A video can now keep an AI description and a local transcription at the same time. When both are put in the message's line, the line says both, for example "..., description: ..., transcription: ...". Deleting the transcription (Alt+Shift+Delete or the context menu) keeps the description; deleting again removes the description. A message deleted for everyone loses both.
  • Long recordings: when a transcription you have just made is over 2 minutes long, the window also has a list called "Moments of the recording (time and text)", one row per moment, each starting with its time, like "[01:05] ...". "Listen from here" (or Enter on a row) jumps the voice message to that second, as long as that message is the one playing; otherwise WinZapp tells you to play it first. "Copy with times" copies the rows with their times, while the text field, Copy and Save never have times. A transcription opened again from the message has only the text, without this list.
  • Vocabulary: the "Vocabulary" field in "Advanced options..." takes names, acronyms and terms, one per line (up to 300 characters), that Whisper should expect so it spells them right. The list stays in this account's settings on this computer, is never written to the log and never goes online. whisper.cpp only uses the terms without accents. It is a hint, not a different model: a transcription already saved is not redone when the list changes (delete it and transcribe again to use new terms).

Online AI

In the Online AI: Transcriptions and Descriptions tab of Settings, you turn the features on (they start off) and enter the API key of one or more providers: Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq, OpenRouter and Mistral. They are tried in the order you set: if one fails or runs out of quota, the next one answers. Each provider has its own window, with the "Use the model recommended by WinZapp" box (WinZapp always uses the model it recommends) or the choice of a fixed model. The "Move up" and "Move down" buttons of the provider list, or Alt+Up Arrow and Alt+Down Arrow on the list, change that order.

WhereWhat it does
Voice message or audio: "Transcribe with online AI"Turns it into text.
Photo or video: "Describe"Describes the image. A video is described from a single image taken from it; the sound is never sent.
Sticker: "Describe sticker"Describes the sticker.
PDF: "Convert to accessible text"Turns it into plain, readable text.
Chat with unread messages: "Summarize unread messages"Summarizes the open chat's unread messages.

Ctrl+Shift+I does all of this on the focused message. The result opens in a window with the text; for photos and videos, you can ask follow-up questions (Ctrl+Enter asks). In the Status tab (Alt+5), the "Describe" button describes photo and video statuses.

In the AI window of a video, the "Understand full video" button tells what is seen (the frames, as in the description) and what is said in one text. If the video already has a saved transcription, WinZapp uses it; otherwise it transcribes the speech on this computer, in a progress window with Cancel, and the sound never leaves the computer. If the speech cannot be transcribed, WinZapp says why and asks whether to describe only the images instead; the answer then says it covers only the images. Before anything is sent, a consent window of its own (asked once each time the video's window is opened, never remembered; a model running on this computer asks nothing) says that the pictures taken from the video and the transcribed text go to the providers it names; the sound does not, and the text may contain private words. A very long text is cut to its first 12,000 characters and the answer says so. Follow-up questions work as in a description, and "Run again (new request)" repeats the request with the speech. The answer is not put in the message line, so it never replaces the saved description or transcription; the transcription made stays saved with the video even if the AI fails.

Settings, Online AI: the new "Providers by task..." button opens a window where each kind of task (voice messages, photos, stickers, videos, PDF, message summary and writing help) can have its own providers in its own order. Check "Use the general order for this task" to keep things as they are; otherwise use "Include or leave out" and the move buttons to build the route. A task with its own route only uses the providers in it (if none is ready, the action is not offered), and every provider still asks for your consent as before.

Settings, Online AI also lists Ollama and LM Studio, two language models that run on your own computer. Select one and use "Set up selected provider": type the "Server address" (the usual one is filled in), use "Get models" to list what is installed, choose a model and, if you like, use "Test connection without sending media". It needs no API key, and nothing leaves your computer while the address is this computer. With no model chosen the provider is not used, and the window and the list row say so. Local models describe pictures, stickers and video frames, summarize chats, help you write and run the quick analysis modes; they do not take voice messages or PDFs. If you set an address that is not on this computer, WinZapp treats it as an online provider and asks for your consent first, and only an https address is accepted for it.

An option in Settings, Online AI, "Types of media handled...", "Show answers as they arrive" (off by default), makes the result window of a description, a PDF or a chat summary fill in whole lines or sentences while the AI is still writing, at most once a second, instead of waiting for the complete answer. The waiting sound stops and the window says once that the answer is arriving; the answer is still read aloud once, when it is complete. If the request fails or you cancel it, the partial text is discarded, and Cancel and Esc close the connection at once. Voice messages and the writing help are not affected.

Result window of a picture, sticker, video or PDF: the new "Quick analysis" choice and "Analyze" button run the same file again with another request: "Extract only the text", "Read receipt, bill or PIX proof" (amount, receiver, date and code or key, without guessing: an unclear value is marked to be confirmed and what could not be read is listed) or "Read table or menu". "Use my own instruction" enables the "Your instruction" field, where you type what you want from the file. The answer replaces the previous one and goes through the same consent.

Settings, Online AI, "Types of media handled...": two new options, both off by default, "Include the caption when describing media" and "Include the quoted message when describing media". When on, that text, written by other people in the chat, is sent with the description request, and a separate consent says so.

Writing help: besides the four actions, "Use my own instruction" lets you type what to do with the draft (for example, "summarize in one line"), and "Suggest a reply draft" asks the AI for a reply based on the last 10 messages of the conversation and your draft. This sends those messages and the names of who wrote them, so it asks for its own consent, every time in locked chats. The suggestion only replaces your draft if you choose "Replace draft".

A video is described from up to five frames spread over it, joined into one picture; the sound is never sent, and the consent window says that several frames, without sound, go out. The model is told the frames are in order and that it cannot know what happens between them or what is said, so the description does not cover that. In Settings, Online AI, "Types of media handled...", the "Describe videos from several frames" box (on by default) can be switched off to send a single frame instead.

Settings, Online AI: the new "Usage summary..." button opens a window that says, for each provider, how many requests it answered and how many failed, with the reason of the last failure, and a "Reset counters" button. Only counts are kept, never the content of a message, a request or an answer.

AI requests: when a provider answers "too many requests" or "unavailable" and asks to wait only a few seconds, WinZapp waits that moment and tries the same provider once more before moving on to the next one. Cancelling still stops everything at once.

On a photo, sticker or video that already has a description saved with its message, Ctrl+Shift+I shows that text at once, without a new request; "Run again (new request)" asks for a fresh description, and follow-up questions stay available. In the same window, "Read text on this computer" reads the text in the picture with Windows' own text recognition: it uses no internet, key or consent, and needs a text recognition language installed in Windows. For a PDF, WinZapp first reads the text the file itself contains, on this computer; only a scanned PDF without text goes on to the AI providers, and "Run again (new request)" asks an AI to also describe the PDF's pictures.

The message summary includes the description already saved with photos, stickers and videos, the options of polls (with votes, when known) and a short excerpt of the message each reply quotes. When something asks you for an answer, a date or a link, the summary ends with a short list of those items.

The "Summarize unread messages" button appears inside the open chat when it has 10 or more unread messages, and Ctrl+Shift+U makes the same request (the shortcut can be changed in the Shortcuts tab of Settings). It uses the providers of this tab and asks for its own consent, separate from the photos and audio one; the details are in "AI message summary", in the section about being inside a chat.

In the result of a transcription or description, "Put transcription in the message line" and "Put description in the message line" keep the text with the message, which then shows it in its own line in the list. In Settings > Transcriptions & Descriptions, "Transcribe received voice messages automatically" and "Describe received pictures, stickers and videos automatically" (both off by default) do this with the online providers, with no window, sound or speech per message; only providers you already authorized are used, and turning one on turns off the local automatic transcription. Each conversation's data window has "transcribe" and "Describe" boxes, and you can choose between all conversations except the ones turned off, or only the ones turned on. To save your quota, you choose how old a received message may be and still be processed (5 minutes, 15, 30 or 1 hour) and a daily ceiling on automatic requests (50 by default, 0 for none); if every provider refuses because of quota or key, the queue pauses for an hour and says so once.

🔒 Privacy
WinZapp asks before sending media to a provider, and asks again each time in locked chats. Your keys are stored encrypted on your computer and are only sent to the provider they belong to. Resetting all keys also clears the remembered send authorizations. A waiting sound plays while the provider works; answers can be read aloud automatically; the text follows the app's language. Very long transcription and PDF texts report a text limit error.

Queue, windows and per-conversation options

With transcription without the progress window, asking for the inline transcription of another message while one is running puts it in a queue: WinZapp says its position and starts it when the previous one ends; the same shortcut on a waiting message takes it out of the queue. A transcription that opens a window is still refused while another runs.

In automatic, WinZapp uses a model already on the computer whenever one fits: on a small graphics card that cannot hold the installed model, it runs that model on the processor instead of asking for a download.

The options of the Local Transcription tab are in windows opened from buttons on it: "Advanced options...", "Saved transcriptions...", "Transcription of statuses...", "Models folder and models in other folders...", and the windows of the CUDA libraries and of whisper.cpp. On the online AI page, the automatic options are in "Automatic transcription and description..." and the kinds of media it handles in "Types of media handled...".

The conversation details have a single choice for automatic transcription: None, Online with AI or Local with Whisper. This way the same message is never transcribed twice, and the choice starts out following the general option until you change it there.

Enter and Space, when set to transcribe, also transcribe audio and video sent as a file.

Mistral uses three different models: the voice message model for audio, Ministral 3 3B by default for photos, stickers and video frames, and mistral-ocr-4 for PDF text extraction. Choose the audio model in "Voice message model": Voxtral Mini Transcribe 3 (the default) or Voxtral Small Transcribe 3, which is more accurate. Both are in preview, so Mistral may change or withdraw them. The Model field and Get models select a vision model only; changing it does not change transcription or OCR. OpenAI and Groq also use fixed audio models, independently of their selected vision model; Groq does not process PDFs here. Gemini uses its selected model for all supported media. Provider availability does not guarantee free usage: review the provider’s included quota and rate limits. Mistral OCR extracts document text; it does not add descriptions of the pictures inside a PDF. On Mistral's free Experiment plan, requests may be used to train its models unless you opt out in your Mistral account; WinZapp says so before sending media to Mistral.

With automatic transcription on, the option "Also transcribe the ones that arrive late" transcribes received messages however old they are (for example, all those that arrive at once when the computer comes back on). Without it, the age limit you chose applies; the time the computer slept or was offline does not count as age. On Windows ARM64 computers, whisper.cpp uses the native ARM64 build, and the build for NVIDIA cards with CUDA 13 is offered as an option.

Writing help

Write a draft and use "Writing help" after "Emoji", or the composer context menu. Help is available for non-empty, editable text. The four writing actions use your configured AI providers; if none is available, only Google translation is offered. Each service requests its own consent before receiving the text. Review the result and choose "Replace draft" to use it. A changed draft or another chat is not overwritten. The message is never sent automatically. Choose an action, then press "Run selected action". Opening or closing this window does not send the draft. The Alt+Shift+H shortcut opens the same window with focus anywhere in the chat and can be changed in the Shortcuts tab of Settings; the field's context menu shows the shortcut in use.
