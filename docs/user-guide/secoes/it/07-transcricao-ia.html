There are two different paths, with different commands: local transcription (Whisper, on your computer, nothing leaves it) and online AI (sends the media to the provider you configure).

Local transcription with Whisper

Turns voice messages, audio and videos into text on your own computer. Everything is configured in the Local Transcription tab of Settings.

ShortcutAction
Alt+Shift+TTranscribes the message and opens the text in a window (copy, save or insert into the message); if a saved transcription exists, shows it
Alt+Shift+NPuts the transcription on the message's row ("..., duration: 0:12, transcription: ...") and reads it aloud, with no window
Alt+Shift+DeleteDeletes the message's saved transcription
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+TTranscribes the selected messages and shows them together
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+NTranscribes the selected ones on their own rows
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+DeleteDeletes the saved transcriptions of the selected ones

In the context menu: "Trascrivi il messaggio in locale con Whisper", "Visualizza la trascrizione", "Trascrivi di nuovo" and "Elimina trascrizione".

  • Components: faster-whisper is the default and comes with WinZapp; whisper.cpp is optional and experimental, and the tab installs it in the version for the processor or for NVIDIA graphics cards. faster-whisper also uses an NVIDIA card once you install the "Librerie CUDA" in the same tab.
  • Models: they are only downloaded when you ask, from tiny to large-v3 and large-v3-turbo; in whisper.cpp there are also smaller versions (8 and 5 bits) and some 32-bit ones. Some models understand only one language (marked in the list). In the list, Ctrl+Space marks models and "Scarica" downloads all the marked ones, one after the other. "Dove eseguire la trascrizione" and the "Calculation precision" stay on Automatic or can be chosen.
  • Reuse what you already have: "Modelli in altre cartelle" uses models downloaded elsewhere without copying them, and "Riutilizza i download da un'altra cartella" points to the CUDA libraries and to the whisper.cpp programs already downloaded. The models folder can be moved, with the models along.
  • Memory: "Mantieni il modello caricato in memoria (faster-whisper e whisper.cpp)" avoids reloading the model for each message. Turned off, the model is released 5 minutes after the last transcription.
  • Per model: the text stored in each message is per model. When you switch models, the message is transcribed again and the previous model's text stays stored. "Cancella le trascrizioni salvate di questo modello" and "Pulisci le trascrizioni salvate di tutti i modelli" clean the cache. The cache can be turned on or off in general and per model.
  • No progress window: the option "Trascrivi senza la finestra di avanzamento: rimani sui messaggi o sugli stati fino a quando il testo non è pronto (lo stesso tasto di scelta rapida annulla l'operazione)" makes Alt+Shift+T and Alt+Shift+N transcribe in the background; the same shortcut on the same message cancels.
  • Automatic: "Trascrivi automaticamente i messaggi vocali e i video ricevuti e inserisci il testo nella riga del messaggio" transcribes each new voice message and video (up to 10 minutes of audio, one at a time, outside locked chats), with no windows or per-message announcements. If transcription is not ready, WinZapp pauses and says why just once.
  • Enter and Space: with "Enter and Space on a voice message or video transcribe it into its line in the list instead of playing it..." (Settings > Audio), Enter and Space transcribe and Alt+Shift+N plays or pauses. Turned off, Enter and Space play and Alt+Shift+N transcribes on the row.
  • Videos: the video's sound is converted and transcribed, with the same options. GIFs are ignored and a video without sound is reported as such.
  • In bulk: WinZapp says how many messages will be transcribed, skipped or reused, transcribes one at a time ("message 3 of 12"), continues if one fails and says at the end how many succeeded, were skipped and failed. The same shortcut or the context menu cancels the queue, and the context menu shows all the transcriptions together, with sender, date and time, to copy or save. With "Sostituisci le scorciatoie con le azioni in blocco quando selezioni chat e messaggi", Alt+Shift+T and Alt+Shift+N also act on the selection.
  • Background downloads: with "Download updates and transcription downloads in the background, without the progress window" (Settings > General), models, whisper.cpp and CUDA download without the progress window, and "Annulla il download in background" stops it.
  • Status: audio and video statuses can be transcribed in the Status tab, with the same keys (see the Status section).

Online AI

In the Online AI: Transcriptions and Descriptions tab of Settings, you turn the features on (they start off) and enter the API key of one or more providers: Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq, OpenRouter and Mistral. They are tried in the order you set: if one fails or runs out of quota, the next one answers. Each provider has its own window, with the "Usa il modello consigliato da WinZapp" box (WinZapp always uses the model it recommends) or the choice of a fixed model.

WhereWhat it does
Voice message or audio: "Trascrivi con l'IA online"Turns it into text.
Photo or video: "Descrizione"Describes the image. A video is described from a single image taken from it; the sound is never sent.
Sticker: "Descrivi l'adesivo"Describes the sticker.
PDF: "Converti in testo accessibile"Turns it into plain, readable text.
Chat with unread messages: "Riassumi non letti"Summarizes the open chat's unread messages.

Ctrl+Shift+I does all of this on the focused message. The result opens in a window with the text; for photos and videos, you can ask follow-up questions (Ctrl+Enter asks). In the Status tab (Alt+5), the "Descrizione" button describes photo and video statuses.

The "Riassumi non letti" button appears inside the open chat when there are enough unread messages, and Alt+Shift+U makes the same request (the shortcut can be changed in the Shortcuts tab of Settings). The minimum is set on the Online AI tab itself (10 by default; 0 turns the feature off). Because the text of those messages leaves the computer, the first time asks for its own consent, separate from the photos and audio one; locked chats never offer a summary. At most 200 messages are sent, the newest ones.

In the result of a transcription or description, "Inserisci la trascrizione nella riga del messaggio" and "Inserisci la descrizione nella riga del messaggio" keep the text with the message, which then shows it in its own line in the list. In Settings > Transcriptions & Descriptions, "Transcribe received voice messages automatically" and "Describe received pictures, stickers and videos automatically" (both off by default) do this with the online providers, with no window, sound or speech per message; only providers you already authorized are used, and turning one on turns off the local automatic transcription. Each conversation's data window has "transcribe" and "Descrizione" boxes, and you can choose between all conversations except the ones turned off, or only the ones turned on. To save your quota, you choose how old a received message may be and still be processed (5 minutes, 15, 30 or 1 hour) and a daily ceiling on automatic requests (50 by default, 0 for none); if every provider refuses because of quota or key, the queue pauses for an hour and says so once.

🔒 Privacy
WinZapp asks before sending media to a provider, and asks again each time in locked chats. Your keys are stored encrypted on your computer and are only sent to the provider they belong to. Resetting all keys also clears the remembered send authorizations. A waiting sound plays while the provider works; answers can be read aloud automatically; the text follows the app's language. Very long transcription and PDF texts report a text limit error.

Queue, windows and per-conversation options

With transcription without the progress window, asking for the inline transcription of another message while one is running puts it in a queue: WinZapp says its position and starts it when the previous one ends; the same shortcut on a waiting message takes it out of the queue. A transcription that opens a window is still refused while another runs.

In automatic, WinZapp uses a model already on the computer whenever one fits: on a small graphics card that cannot hold the installed model, it runs that model on the processor instead of asking for a download.

The options of the Local Transcription tab are in windows opened from buttons on it: "Opzioni Avanzate...", "Trascrizioni salvate...", "Trascrizioni degli stati.", "Cartella dei modelli e quelli in altre cartelle...", and the windows of the CUDA libraries and of whisper.cpp. The automatic options of online AI are in "Abilita le funzionalità di IA (trascrizione e descrizione)", on its page.

The conversation details have a single choice for automatic transcription: None, Online with AI or Local with Whisper. This way the same message is never transcribed twice, and the choice starts out following the general option until you change it there.

Enter and Space, when set to transcribe, also transcribe audio and video sent as a file.

Mistral uses three different models: the voice message model for audio, Ministral 3 3B by default for photos, stickers and video frames, and mistral-ocr-4 for PDF text extraction. Choose the audio model in "modello di messaggio vocale": Voxtral Mini Transcribe 3 (the default) or Voxtral Small Transcribe 3, which is more accurate. Both are in preview, so Mistral may change or withdraw them. The Model field and Get models select a vision model only; changing it does not change transcription or OCR. OpenAI and Groq also use fixed audio models, independently of their selected vision model; Groq does not process PDFs here. Gemini uses its selected model for all supported media. Provider availability does not guarantee free usage: review the provider’s included quota and rate limits. Mistral OCR extracts document text; it does not add descriptions of the pictures inside a PDF. On Mistral's free Experiment plan, requests may be used to train its models unless you opt out in your Mistral account; WinZapp says so before sending media to Mistral.
