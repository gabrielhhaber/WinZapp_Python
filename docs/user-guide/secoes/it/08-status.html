The Status tab (Alt+5) shows your contacts' statuses and lets you post your own.

  • View: the list shows the statuses; Enter opens one. Photo and video statuses open in the viewer (separate or in the player inside the tab) and Ctrl+Left/Right Arrow goes to the previous/next status.
  • React: "Like ❤️".
  • Reply: "Reply to status" opens the reply field; Enter sends ("Reply sent.").
  • Post: "Post text as status" and "Send media as status". "Status posted successfully." confirms. Your own statuses are in "My status".
  • Save and describe: "Save as..." and "Describe" (online AI) work on photo and video statuses.
  • Copy: Ctrl+C copies the status text. F5 refreshes the list.
  • Voice: in replies, Ctrl+R records a voice message (Ctrl+Shift+P pauses, Ctrl+P listens, Ctrl+Shift+D discards) and Ctrl+. opens the emoji picker.
  • Transcribe audio and video statuses (local): Alt+Shift+T opens the text in the result window (or the saved transcription); Alt+Shift+N puts it on the status row and reads it aloud; Alt+Shift+Delete deletes the saved transcription. The "My status" window also accepts these keys on your own audio and video statuses. In Settings > Local Transcription there are options just for statuses: automatically transcribe received ones, Enter and Space transcribing on the row, and saving the transcriptions together with the status (cache deleted along with it when the status expires), plus the "Clear saved local transcriptions of statuses" button.

Who can see your statuses is set in File > WhatsApp settings > Privacy.

Posting again and deleting

WinZapp posts one status at a time; asking to post again while one is still going only says it is being posted. A status that failed or was not confirmed stays in the list right after "My status", with "Try posting again" and "Discard" in its menu (Ctrl+Shift+Y tries again); nothing is posted again on its own. In "My status", the Delete key or the "Delete status" button deletes a status for everyone, after the same confirmation as WhatsApp.
