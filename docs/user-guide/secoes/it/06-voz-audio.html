Ctrl+Shift+HRecord microphone and computer audio
  • Voice message recording channels: There is one record button. In Settings > Audio, "Voice message recording channels" offers "Automatic (use the device's channels)" (the default), "Mono (mix left and right)" and "Stereo (when available)". Automatic and Stereo keep two channels when the device supports them and use mono otherwise. Mono averages left and right into one channel: use it for a microphone connected only to INPUT 1 or INPUT 2 of an audio interface. A single active input is quieter after averaging, and no automatic gain is applied. In Automatic, a recording with one side completely silent goes out in mono, taken from the side with sound. The choice is fixed when recording starts and also applies to the paused preview. It does not affect calls or microphone + computer audio recording. Stereo voice messages also play on iPhone.
  • Microphone + computer audio: records in a single message your voice and what the computer plays (a video, music, the screen reader). During the recording, two sliders adjust the volume of the other sounds and of NVDA (without NVDA, the second one is disabled); they only apply to the recording and are kept per account. Before starting, WinZapp warns that everything that plays will go into the recording, with "Don't show again" (the option "Warn before recording computer audio" brings the warning back). If WinZapp stalls for a second or two (a slow or busy computer), the sound of that moment is skipped and the recording goes on. The recording is sent as audio.
  • Recording announcements can be silenced; the screen reader does not start saying "Send" before being interrupted.

  • The microphone and speaker used are in Settings > Audio.
  • With Neonize, the voice message is also delivered to contacts on Android.
  • If the computer's file-type library (libmagic) fails to load, Neonize recognizes the type from the file itself and the voice message goes out normally.
  • Mono voice messages are recorded at 64 kbps by default and stereo ones at 128 kbps. In Settings > Audio, "Voice message bitrate (kbps)" (mono) and "Stereo voice message bitrate (kbps)" let you choose from 32 to 256 kbps.

Listen

  • On voice and audio messages, Enter or Space play and pause. In Settings > Audio you can tell voice messages apart from audio files.
  • Speed: Alt+, slows down and Alt+. speeds up. The default speed is set in Settings > Audio > Audio playback.
  • Forward and back: Shift+Right/Left Arrow moves 5 seconds; Shift+Page Down/Up moves 1 minute; Shift+Home and Shift+End go to the start and the end of what is playing.
  • The following voice messages play in sequence, unless you clear "Automatically play voice messages in sequence" in Settings > User interface. During the sequence, the focused row waits to update its state, so the screen reader does not read over the audio.
  • Ctrl+Alt+Shift+P pauses or resumes the audio that is playing, from any panel.
  • If the output device changes (headphones unplugged, for example), WinZapp switches to the default device and plays the message the first time.
  • If the sound card is not available (for example, when the computer wakes up), WinZapp tries to restore the audio output when a playback fails; if it still cannot play, it tells you through the screen reader.
  • In Settings, there is an option not to mark audio as played.

Switch the audio device on the spot

Ctrl+Alt+Shift+H opens the list of playback devices and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+G the list of recording devices, numbered 1 to 0: press the number to switch right away, or use the arrows and Enter. The switch applies to sounds, voice messages and calls (a call in progress changes without dropping), for all open accounts, until you close WinZapp. The devices chosen in Settings do not change.

Attiva la riduzione del rumore del microfono in Impostazioni > Audio. Apri le impostazioni del riduttore per scaricare il modello una sola volta, scegliere il processore (CPU, CUDA 12/13 su Windows o CoreML su Mac) e gestire i componenti necessari. L’audio viene elaborato sul computer. Nella pagina Audio scegli il motore, il momento e l’azione in caso di errore:

Motore di riduzione del rumore
Neurale / Basilare (noise gate)
Elabora le note vocali
All'invio / Durante la registrazione
Quando la riduzione del rumore non riesce
Invia con riduzione del rumore basilare (predefinito) / Mantieni il messaggio vocale in sospeso per riprovare a inviarlo

Mantieni l’elaborazione all’invio, predefinita, oppure scegli quella durante la registrazione per lavorare in background mentre registri. Quest’ultima è disponibile solo con CPU o con il percorso CoreML interno al programma. Scegliendo CUDA viene disattivata e si elabora all’invio; non è disponibile l’elaborazione CUDA durante la registrazione. Il percorso CoreML durante la registrazione non è stato validato nell’uso reale. Se l’elaborazione neurale fallisce, l’impostazione predefinita invia con la riduzione di base. Scegli di lasciare il messaggio in sospeso per conservarlo: riprova manualmente dal menu contestuale oppure scegli esplicitamente "Invia senza riduzione del rumore".
