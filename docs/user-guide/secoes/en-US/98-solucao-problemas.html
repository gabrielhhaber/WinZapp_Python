SituationWhat to do
WinZapp asks you to pair againPair with number or QR code. Your chats are only deleted if you connect a different number. If it happened for no reason, keep log.log and shutdown_audit.log before opening it again.
Messages do not arrive or the list looks stuckWait for the sync progress; use Shift+F5 in the chat or F5 for all. Also check that offline mode is not on. If the list keeps syncing again and again, update WinZapp: a fix lets the chat list finish syncing.
Local transcription on the NVIDIA graphics card fails or gets stuckUpdate WinZapp: a fix loads the CUDA libraries on Windows before the graphics card is used. If they cannot be loaded, WinZapp says so and offers to transcribe on the processor instead.
WinZapp freezes when sending or receiving large filesUpdate WinZapp: a fix stops a received file from being downloaded along with the message notification and stops send and download progress from piling up screen updates.
The "chat not found" error appears when sending to an existing conversationUpdate WinZapp: a fix makes sending messages and attachments use the conversation WhatsApp has already loaded.
My account still shows as online after I left the windowUpdate WinZapp: your online status now follows the window (focus, minimized, hidden in the tray, background start), and a failed offline update is retried every 20 seconds.
Calls do not startRestart WinZapp or use Help > Force reinstall WPPConnect. WinZapp keeps the list of WhatsApp Web versions up to date by itself.
The internal browser does not openUsually an antivirus removed part of it. WinZapp warns and explains; use Force reinstall WPPConnect.
A message was sent but WinZapp did not confirmCheck the chat before resending; WinZapp does not resend by itself in these cases.
"Use WhatsApp on your phone to see older messages"Those messages exist only on the phone.
An error appears when installing or updating WPPConnectThe error comes in a read-only text field, to read and copy; send it when reporting the problem.
Local transcription does not workIn the Local Transcription tab, confirm that a model is downloaded and ready. WinZapp says why when transcription is not ready.
