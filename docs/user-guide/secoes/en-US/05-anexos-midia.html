Photos, videos, audio, documents, contacts and polls are sent from the attachment menu; received media is opened, saved and described right in the message list.

Send attachments

Ctrl+Shift+A opens the attachment menu. The types are photos, videos, audio, documents, contact and, last, Poll.

  • Size: documents up to 2 GB; photos, videos and audio up to 1 GB, which are WhatsApp's limits.
  • Caption: the caption field takes several lines. Enter sends the attachment with its caption; Shift+Enter or Ctrl+Enter start a new line.
  • Audio files: a stereo or surround (5.1, 7.1) WAV or OGG Vorbis arrives with all channels, as ordinary audio, and keeps the sample rate up to 48 kHz. A file with more than 8 channels is sent unchanged as a document. Audio files pasted into the message field with Ctrl+V can go as audio or as a document (Settings > Audio; the default is audio).
  • Attach contact: the list opens with the search field focused, filters by name (including surname) and number, and has no duplicates.
  • Receiving a contact: an attached contact you receive shows the name and the number, which you can read, copy and use to chat with the person.
  • Poll: choose Poll and enter the question, up to 12 options and whether multiple answers are allowed.
  • Sent documents already show the file size in the list; while a document is downloading, the row shows progress, for example "35% (13.8 MB of 39.4 MB)".
  • Downloaded files always have the same size as the original, including those whose size is a multiple of 16 bytes, such as an APK, which Android installs normally.

Open, save and describe

On a message with media, Enter opens it (photo in the viewer, audio and video play, document opens in the default program). Space plays and pauses audio and video, but never opens links, documents or the viewer. After "Open" there are the buttons, reachable with Tab, "Save as..." (Ctrl+Shift+S), "Show in folder" (Ctrl+Enter, when you saved a copy) and "Describe" (Ctrl+Shift+I, when the AI features are on). The context menu has the same items.

A video message (the video recorded with the camera button in WhatsApp, sent instead of a voice message) appears in the list as "video message" and works like any video: Enter plays it, and Save as, the duration and the rest of the menu are the same.

The folder where the save windows open is chosen in Settings > Storage: the last folder used, Downloads or a folder of your own.

Media viewer

A viewer in its own window (if you turn it on in the settings for chat videos and statuses) has play and pause, forward and back 10 seconds (Alt+A and Alt+V), position, volume and speed. Ctrl+Shift+S saves and Ctrl+Shift+I describes.

Media tab

In Chat info and Group info, the Media tab lists photos, videos, audio, voice messages, documents and links, with a filter by type and by downloaded or not downloaded, loading of the full history, and the actions open, save as and delete on each item. Audio and voice messages are separate filters.

Automatic download

In Settings > Storage, "Automatically download media" turns on media download, and you choose the types. Unchecking it stops what is in progress; checking it (or checking one more type) starts downloading right away and also applies to the media of messages that arrive while WinZapp is open. Even with everything off, opening a media item manually downloads it right then. "Download media" (Sync menu, Ctrl+Alt+Shift+B) triggers the download whenever you want. WinZapp announces progress by voice, such as "Downloading media: 30 of 120 processed (8 downloaded)".

Settings > Storage > "Media cleanup" frees disk space by deleting the downloaded media files of the conversations you choose; the messages stay. You pick the conversations (with a "Select all" row and a groups/individual filter), the media types (photos and stickers, videos, audios, voice messages, documents) and a minimum size in MB. "Gather data" only reads and tells how many files and how many MB match, per type; "Proceed with cleanup" asks for confirmation (No is the default) and then deletes exactly what was previewed. Files you saved with Save As are never touched, nor are the media of unsent messages or locked chats. The cleaned files are remembered, so automatic downloads and syncs do not fetch them again, and an explicit download says so; only a logout or a new pairing forgets them, not F5.
