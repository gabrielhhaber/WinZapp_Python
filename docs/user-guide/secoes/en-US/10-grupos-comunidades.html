Chat info and group info

Ctrl+Shift+D (or "Chat info" in the context menu) opens a window with tabs. All buttons and fields have a shortcut letter (Alt+letter).

  • Contact: Overview tab with name, the profile name the contact chose, verified name and whether the account is a business account, whether notifications are muted (and until when), the disappearing messages timer and the notice that messages and calls are end-to-end encrypted. The "Profile picture" button (Alt+F) shows the picture, and "Open in external viewer" (Alt+A) opens it in the Windows default program; if there is no picture, or the person does not allow you to see it, WinZapp says so. There is also the Groups in common tab (Enter opens the group) and the Media tab.
  • Group: Overview tab (name, description, group picture, whether only admins send messages), Participants tab (Enter on a participant opens the private chat with them; "Add member" accepts contacts or an extra number) and Media tab. The data appears as soon as the group answers and missing names and numbers are filled in right after. On the Media tab, Ctrl+C on a document, photo, video or audio copies the file to the clipboard, as in the message list.
  • Notices: in groups where only admins send messages, the message field is read-only and WinZapp says so.

Create a group and invitations

In "New chat", create a group by entering a name and participants. In Invitation to the group, the invitation link and the QR code can be viewed, copied ("Copy link"), saved ("Save QR code...") and reset with confirmation ("Reset invitation link..."). In Join group by link, paste the link (including those that end in ?mode=...), see the "Preview group" and choose "Join group or request approval". The results of pending approval and of unconfirmed joining are reported separately.

In Settings > General, "Let Windows open WhatsApp links (group invitations and chats) with WinZapp" registers WinZapp for whatsapp: links: when you use the join button on the invitation page opened in the browser, Windows hands the invitation to WinZapp, even when it is closed. The same way, when you open a wa.me or api.whatsapp.com link (from a website, an airline or a shop, for example), WinZapp opens the chat with that number, even when it is not in your contacts, puts the message from the link in the message field and leaves the focus there, unsent. The installed version registers itself on its first launch, so WinZapp is listed under Default apps in Windows; in the portable version, turn the option on yourself.

Join requests (admins)

In groups where you are an admin, the "Pending join requests" button lists who is waiting (name or number, how the request arrived and when). "Approve" and "Reject" answer the selected request; "Refresh" checks again. WinZapp only says the request was answered once it has left the list on WhatsApp.

Communities (Alt+8)

The Communities panel (or "New chat" > "Communities") lists the communities you are in. When you choose one, you see the description, the admins and the groups of it you joined, with the announcements group and the general group identified; Enter or "Open group" goes to the group. If you administer the community: "Add groups", "Remove group from the community", "Promote to community admin", "Demote community admin" and "Deactivate community". "New community" creates one with a name, description and the groups you choose. After each action WinZapp reads the communities again and only says it finished when WhatsApp shows the change; otherwise it tells you the request was sent but is not confirmed, or that it was refused.

Channels (Alt+9)

The Channels window (Alt+9, or the Channels item of the navigation list) shows "My channels", the channels you follow, or "Find channels": type in "Search channels" and press Enter; with no text, WhatsApp's recommended channels appear. "Details" holds the description, the number of followers, whether you follow, administer or own the channel, and its link. Enter or "Open channel" opens the channel to read its updates. The "Follow" and "Unfollow", "Mute notifications" and "Unmute notifications", "Copy link", "Follow by link..." (paste the channel link) and "Create channel..." buttons work on any channel; "Edit channel..." appears on channels you administer and "Delete channel" on your own. Showing channels in the chat list is still an option of Settings > Interface > Chats. In "Create channel..." and "Edit channel...", Reactions sets who may react to the updates, as in WhatsApp Web's channel settings: any emoji, default emojis only or no reactions; the Details say the channel's choice. In a channel you administer, "Insights..." reads its statistics for the last 30 days, as WhatsApp Web shows them: accounts reached, followers, net followers, new followers and unfollows, reach among followers and others, and by country. WhatsApp has them from 100 followers on. Where WhatsApp offers it to your account, "Edit channel..." also has "Show admin profile on updates", as in WhatsApp Web's channel info (WPPConnect).

In "Find channels", the Category and View choices narrow the directory as WhatsApp Web's options do: pick a category (or "All categories") and a view (recommended, trending, popular or new) and press Search or Enter. With text in "Search channels", the search stays within the chosen category. In the channels you administer, the message field opens when you open the channel: type and send to post a text update; photos, videos and polls can be posted too, with the same attach and create poll commands as in a chat (no voice notes or other files). From the message menu, or Ctrl+Shift+P, you pin or unpin an update, and the pinned ones show under the "Pinned messages" button. The Channels window's "Admins and followers..." button says how many admins the channel has; its owner can also use "Invite admin..." (pick the contact and, if you like, a message: the invite reaches their chat and they become an admin when they accept it) and "Cancel admin invite...". That window lists the owner, the admins, the pending invites and the followers: the owner can make a follower an admin, cancel an invite, remove an admin or make an admin the owner, and an admin can stop being one.

In "Find channels", picking another category or list searches again. The Channels window and the "Admins and followers..." window update by themselves when you follow, leave or mute a channel, when your role, a channel's name or description changes and when who administers it changes, from the phone too. F5 reads the list again (to see new followers, for example); the shortcut can be changed in Settings. In the channels you administer, your text updates can be edited from the message menu or with Alt+E, within WhatsApp's edit window. In a channel opened from the Channels window, Esc goes back to that window as you left it: the same list, search, category and selected channel. A channel opens on a whole page of its newest updates, read from WhatsApp's server.

Mentions and replies

A person mentioned by name together with @all keeps the link; the mention of you, in the notification and in the preview, uses the word chosen in "How should I be referred to?". In Settings > Interface > Messages, "Hide phone numbers of unnamed senders in messages" makes messages from people without a name start straight with the content. In Settings > Interface > Chats, "Precede names of unsaved contacts with a tilde (~) in conversations and messages" (on by default) shows a tilde before the name of anyone you have not saved, in conversations and in messages.

Groups of a community

In the list of communities, Enter goes to the community's groups, and "Open the most recent group" opens the group with the latest activity, saying its name. "Filter groups" shows all groups, the groups you are in or the ones you are not in; a group you are not in says so in its line. On one of those, "Join group" joins it or, when the group needs approval, "Request to join" sends the request, which "Cancel join request" withdraws.

Invitations to follow a channel or to be one of its admins show in the chat as "Invitation to follow the channel..." (or "to be an admin of the channel..."), with the inviter's words. Enter opens the channel, where you can follow it.
