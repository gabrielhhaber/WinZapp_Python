There are two different paths, with different commands: local transcription (Whisper, on your computer, nothing leaves it) and online AI (sends the media to the provider you configure).

Local transcription with Whisper

Turns voice messages, audio and videos into text on your own computer. Everything is configured in the Local Transcription tab of Settings.

ShortcutAction
Alt+Shift+TTranscribes the message and opens the text in a window (copy, save or insert into the message); if a saved transcription exists, shows it
Alt+Shift+NPuts the transcription on the message's row ("..., duration: 0:12, transcription: ...") and reads it aloud, with no window
Alt+Shift+DeleteDeletes the message's saved transcription
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+TTranscribes the selected messages and shows them together
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+NTranscribes the selected ones on their own rows
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+DeleteDeletes the saved transcriptions of the selected ones

In the context menu: "Transcribe message locally with Whisper", "View transcription", "Transcribe again" and "Delete transcription".

  • Components: faster-whisper is the default and comes with WinZapp; whisper.cpp is optional and experimental, and the tab installs it in the version for the processor or for NVIDIA graphics cards. faster-whisper also uses an NVIDIA card once you install the "CUDA libraries" in the same tab.
  • Models: they are only downloaded when you ask, from tiny to large-v3 and large-v3-turbo; in whisper.cpp there are also smaller versions (8 and 5 bits) and some 32-bit ones. Some models understand only one language (marked in the list). In the list, Ctrl+Space marks models and "Download" downloads all the marked ones, one after the other. "Where to run the transcription" and the "Calculation precision" stay on Automatic or can be chosen.
  • Reuse what you already have: "Models in other folders" uses models downloaded elsewhere without copying them, and "Reuse downloads from another folder" points to the CUDA libraries and to the whisper.cpp programs already downloaded. The models folder can be moved, with the models along.
  • Memory: "Keep the model loaded in memory (faster-whisper and whisper.cpp)" avoids reloading the model for each message. Turned off, the model is released 5 minutes after the last transcription.
  • Per model: the text stored in each message is per model. When you switch models, the message is transcribed again and the previous model's text stays stored. "Clear this model's saved transcriptions" and "Clear saved transcriptions of all models" clean the cache. The cache can be turned on or off in general and per model.
  • No progress window: the option "Transcribe without the progress window: stay on the messages or statuses until the text is ready (the same shortcut cancels)" makes Alt+Shift+T and Alt+Shift+N transcribe in the background; the same shortcut on the same message cancels.
  • Automatic: "Automatically transcribe received voice messages and videos and put the text in the message line" transcribes each new voice message and video (up to 10 minutes of audio, one at a time, outside locked chats), with no windows or per-message announcements. If transcription is not ready, WinZapp pauses and says why just once.
  • Enter and Space: with "Enter and Space on a voice message or video transcribe it into its line in the list instead of playing it..." (Settings > Audio), Enter and Space transcribe and Alt+Shift+N plays or pauses. Turned off, Enter and Space play and Alt+Shift+N transcribes on the row.
  • Videos: the video's sound is converted and transcribed, with the same options. GIFs are ignored and a video without sound is reported as such.
  • In bulk: WinZapp says how many messages will be transcribed, skipped or reused, transcribes one at a time ("message 3 of 12"), continues if one fails and says at the end how many succeeded, were skipped and failed. The same shortcut or the context menu cancels the queue, and the context menu shows all the transcriptions together, with sender, date and time, to copy or save. With "Replace shortcuts with bulk actions when selecting chats and messages", Alt+Shift+T and Alt+Shift+N also act on the selection.
  • Background downloads: with "Download updates and transcription downloads in the background, without the progress window" (Settings > General), models, whisper.cpp and CUDA download without the progress window, and "Cancel download in the background" stops it.
  • Status: audio and video statuses can be transcribed in the Status tab, with the same keys (see the Status section).

Online AI

In the Online AI: Transcriptions and Descriptions tab of Settings, you turn the features on (they start off) and enter the API key of one or more providers: Gemini, OpenAI, Claude, Groq and OpenRouter. They are tried in the order you set: if one fails or runs out of quota, the next one answers. Each provider has its own window, with the "Use the model recommended by WinZapp" box (WinZapp always uses the model it recommends) or the choice of a fixed model.

WhereWhat it does
Voice message or audio: "Transcribe with online AI"Turns it into text.
Photo or video: "Describe"Describes the image. A video is described from a single image taken from it; the sound is never sent.
Sticker: "Describe sticker"Describes the sticker.
PDF: "Convert to accessible text"Turns it into plain, readable text.

Ctrl+Shift+I does all of this on the focused message. The result opens in a window with the text; for photos and videos, you can ask follow-up questions (Ctrl+Enter asks). In the Status tab (Alt+5), the "Describe" button describes photo and video statuses.

In the result of a transcription or description, "Put transcription in the message line" and "Put description in the message line" keep the text with the message, which then shows it in its own line in the list. In Settings > Transcriptions & Descriptions, "Transcribe received voice messages automatically" and "Describe received pictures, stickers and videos automatically" (both off by default) do this with the online providers, with no window, sound or speech per message; only providers you already authorized are used, and turning one on turns off the local automatic transcription. Each conversation's data window has "transcribe" and "describe" boxes, and you can choose between all conversations except the ones turned off, or only the ones turned on. To save your quota, you choose how old a received message may be and still be processed (5 minutes, 15, 30 or 1 hour) and a daily ceiling on automatic requests (50 by default, 0 for none); if every provider refuses because of quota or key, the queue pauses for an hour and says so once.

🔒 Privacy
WinZapp asks before sending media to a provider, and asks again each time in locked chats. Your keys are stored encrypted on your computer and are only sent to the provider they belong to. Resetting all keys also clears the remembered send authorizations. A waiting sound plays while the provider works; answers can be read aloud automatically; the text follows the app's language. Very long transcription and PDF texts report a text limit error.
